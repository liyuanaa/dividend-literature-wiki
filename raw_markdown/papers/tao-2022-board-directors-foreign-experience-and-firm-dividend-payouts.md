## Abstract (English)

We find that Chinese-listed firms are more likely to pay dividends and implement larger payout ratios when their directors have foreign experience. This finding remains after using various methods to address potential endogeneity concerns. The positive association is stronger for lowgrowth firms, non-state-owned firms, and when directors obtain foreign experience in countries with stronger investor protection. The effect of directors' foreign experience on dividend payouts is also more pronounced for independent directors than for executive directors. Directors with foreign experience appear to overcome the weak investor protection environment as the effects are more pronounced among poorly governed firms and before the regulatory dividend reforms in 2013. Overall, our findings identify a mechanism through which better governance practices are propagated.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们发现，当董事拥有海外经历时，中国上市企业更可能支付股利并实施更大的支付率。这一发现在使用各种方法处理潜在内生性顾虑之后依然成立。这一正相关关系对于低成长企业、非国有企业，以及董事在投资者保护更强的国家获得海外经历的情形更强。董事海外经历对股利支付的影响，对于独立董事也比执行董事更为显著。拥有海外经历的董事似乎克服了薄弱的投资者保护环境，因为这些效应在治理不佳的企业中、以及在2013年监管股利改革之前更为显著。总体而言，我们的发现识别出了一种更好治理实践得以传播的机制。
