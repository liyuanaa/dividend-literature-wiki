## Abstract (English)

We analyze a uniquely constructed data set of open market share repurchases across a sample of European firms. We find that the announcement date market reaction is lower than that in the US, mainly because of (i) the relatively large number of recurring announcements which generate significantly lower returns than the initial announcements of intention to repurchase shares; (ii) the rather low market reaction in France, due probably to specific governance and corporate cultural issues; and (iii) the regulatory reform that allowed UK firms to keep the repurchased shares as treasury stock, which decreased their market impact. Across our countries, taxation, shareholder protection, and the European Union's Market Abuse Directive do not affect significantly the market valuation of repurchases. Our results imply that ultimately, domestic institutional specificities and reforms play significant roles in the market valuation and popularity of share repurchases. (C) 2014 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们分析了一个独特构建的欧洲企业公开市场股份回购数据集。我们发现，公告日的市场反应低于美国，主要原因是：（i）经常性公告数量相对较多，其收益显著低于回购股份意向的初次公告；（ii）法国的市场反应相当低，这可能是由于特定的公司治理和企业文化问题；以及（iii）允许英国企业将回购的股份作为库存股保留的监管改革，降低了其市场影响。在我们考察的各个国家中，税收、股东保护以及欧盟《市场滥用指令》并未显著影响回购的市场估值。我们的结果意味着，归根结底，国内的制度特殊性和改革在股份回购的市场估值和普及程度中发挥着重要作用。
