## Abstract (English)

This study investigates the informational effect of stock liquidity on dividend payouts. Using a sample of Chinese listed firms during 2000-2014, we find a positive relation between stock liquidity and dividend payouts. This result is robust to the use of alternative measures of liquidity, and holds after we control for endogeneity concerns. In accord with our hypothesis that stock liquidity provides information and increases insiders' incentive to pay out dividends, we find that the positive relation between stock liquidity and dividend payouts is more pronounced when the information environment is opaque, and when conflict between controlling shareholders and minority investors is severe. Further, market reactions to regulatory stipulations requiring dividend payouts are more favorable for firms with low stock liquidity, suggesting that legal provisions and regulations are substitutes for stock liquidity. Finally, we rule out several alternative explanations concerning the governance of non-controlling blockholders and the alleviation of manager-shareholder agency conflict. (C) 2016 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本研究考察股票流动性对股利支付的信息效应。使用2000—2014年间中国上市企业的样本，我们发现股票流动性与股利支付之间存在正相关关系。这一结果在使用替代性流动性度量时是稳健的，并在控制内生性顾虑后依然成立。与我们的假设（即股票流动性提供信息并增强内部人支付股利的动机）相一致，我们发现，当信息环境不透明、以及当控股股东与中小投资者之间的冲突严重时，股票流动性与股利支付之间的正相关关系更为显著。此外，对于股票流动性低的企业，市场对要求支付股利的监管规定的反应更为积极，这表明法律条款和监管是股票流动性的替代品。最后，我们排除了若干关于非控股大股东治理以及缓解经理人-股东代理冲突的替代性解释。
