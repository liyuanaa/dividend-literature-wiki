## Abstract (English)

We propose and test the hypothesis that overconfident-CEOs, with upwardly-biased estimates of own firm-value, are more predisposed to repurchasing stock. An implication is that the stock-market, recognizing overconfident-CEO behavior, will react less positively to repurchase announcements. The hypothesis is strongly supported: Overconfident managers repurchase stock at lower levels of cash holdings, and respond more to stock-price declines. Entrenchment exacerbates this behavior. Interestingly, institutional investors appear to encourage repurchases, perhaps to curb excessive investment. Overconfident-CEOs are also more likely to substitute repurchases for dividends or capital expenditure. Consistent with our hypothesis, the stock-market reaction to these share repurchase announcements is less positive. (C) 2018 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们提出并检验这一假设：对自身企业价值有向上偏误估计的过度自信CEO更倾向于回购股票。其一个含义是，认识到过度自信CEO行为的股票市场将对回购公告作出不那么正面的反应。该假设得到有力支持：过度自信的经理人在更低的现金持有水平上回购股票，并对股价下跌反应更强烈。固守地位加剧了这种行为。有趣的是，机构投资者似乎会鼓励回购，或许是为了抑制过度投资。过度自信的CEO也更可能用回购替代股利或资本支出。与我们的假设一致，股票市场对这些股份回购公告的反应不那么正面。
