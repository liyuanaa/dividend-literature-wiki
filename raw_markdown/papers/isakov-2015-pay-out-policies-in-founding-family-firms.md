## Abstract (English)

This article analyses founding family influence on pay-out policies for Swiss listed firms over the period 2003-2010. We hypothesise that family firms have different incentives and characteristics that affect pay-out decisions and propose three possible explanations: agency theory, reputation building and family income needs. Our results show that founding family firms display significantly higher dividend pay-outs relative to companies with other ownership structures. We also examine specific family characteristics and document that a family's stake, active involvement and generation play an important role in determining pay-out policies. Our findings appear to be consistent with the family income hypothesis and to some extent with reputational concerns. (C) 2015 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文分析了2003至2010年期间创始家族对瑞士上市公司派现政策的影响。我们假设家族企业具有不同的激励和特征，这些会影响派现决策，并提出三种可能的解释：代理理论、声誉建立和家族收入需求。我们的结果表明，创始家族企业相比其他所有权结构的公司显示出显著更高的股利派发。我们还考察了具体的家族特征，并记录到家族的持股份额、积极参与和代际在决定派现政策方面发挥了重要作用。我们的发现似乎与家族收入假说一致，并在一定程度上与声誉考量一致。
