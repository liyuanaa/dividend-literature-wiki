## Abstract (English)

This paper uses a dynamic partial equilibrium model to explain a puzzle of dividend smoothing. In contrast to the Modigliani-Miller theory, I show that firm value depends on payout policy. The analysis implies that firms with more stable dividend stream are more valuable. This explains why dividends are rigid over time. A volatile component of dividends is introduced to reduce the likelihood of dividend omission in bad times while keeping the same historical average dividends. I show that the empirically observed positive relation between dividends and future firm performance is a statistical artifact driven by dividend smoothing. Thus, the empirical tests of dividend signaling theory might be misspecified. (C) 2014 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文使用一个动态局部均衡模型来解释股利平滑之谜。与Modigliani-Miller理论相反，我表明企业价值取决于支付政策。分析表明，拥有更稳定股利流的企业更有价值。这解释了为何股利随时间推移是刚性的。引入股利的波动性成分是为了降低在困难时期取消股利的可能性，同时保持相同的历史平均股利。我表明，实证观察到的股利与未来企业业绩之间的正向关系是由股利平滑所驱动的一种统计假象。因此，对股利信号理论的实证检验可能是设定错误的。
