## Abstract (English)

This paper examines the relative efficiency of UK credit unions. Radial and non-radial measures of input cost efficiency plus associated scale efficiency measures are computed for a selection of input output specifications. Both measures highlighted that UK credit unions have considerable scope for efficiency gains. It was mooted that the documented high levels of inefficiency may be indicative of the fact that credit unions, based on clearly defined and non-overlapping common bonds, are not in competition with each other for market share. Credit unions were also highlighted as suffering from a considerable degree of scale inefficiency with the majority of scale inefficient credit unions subject to decreasing returns to scale. The latter aspect highlights that the UK Government's goal of larger credit unions must be accompanied by greater regulatory freedom if inefficiency is to be avoided. One of the advantages of computing non-radial measures is that an insight into potential over- or under-expenditure on specific inputs can be obtained through a comparison of the non-radial measure of efficiency with the associated radial measure. Two interesting findings emerged, the first that UK credit unions over-spend on dividend payments and the second that they under-spend on labour costs. (C) 2002 Elsevier Science B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文考察了英国信用合作社的相对效率。针对若干投入产出规格，计算了投入成本效率的径向和非径向度量以及相关的规模效率度量。两种度量都凸显出，英国信用合作社在效率提升方面有相当大的空间。有人提出，所记录的高水平低效率可能表明，基于清晰界定且互不重叠的共同纽带的信用合作社彼此之间并不为市场份额而竞争。研究还凸显出信用合作社遭受相当程度的规模低效率，大多数规模低效率的信用合作社处于规模报酬递减状态。后一方面凸显出，英国政府关于扩大信用合作社规模的目标，若要避免低效率，就必须伴随更大的监管自由。计算非径向度量的优势之一是，通过将非径向效率度量与相关的径向度量进行比较，可以洞察在特定投入上可能存在的超支或不足支出。出现了两个有趣的发现，其一是英国信用合作社在股利支付上超支，其二是它们在劳动成本上支出不足。
