## Abstract (English)

Because the personal tax treatments of interest and dividend income likely affect the relative cost of debt and equity financing, a sharp change in tax treatment could affect firms' optimal leverage. This paper examines the effect of the 2003 equity income tax cut on firms' debt usage. Because this tax cut affected only individual investors, we can use a difference-in-differences method to identify the effect of personal tax on firms' leverage. Previous research has found that the 2003 tax cut encouraged dividend payouts and reduced the cost of equity, but it provides no link to equilibrium leverage ratios. We estimate that the tax cut causes the affected firms' leverage to decrease by about 5 percentage points. Furthermore, we show that the effects of the tax cut are stronger for firms with lower marginal corporate tax rates and for firms that are not financially constrained, consistent with our theoretical predictions. Overall, we find strong evidence that personal tax is an important determinant of firms' optimal leverage. (C) 2013 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

由于利息和股利收入的个人税收待遇很可能会影响债务融资和权益融资的相对成本，税收待遇的急剧变化可能会影响公司的最优杠杆。本文考察2003年权益收入减税对公司债务使用的影响。由于这次减税仅影响个人投资者，我们可以使用双重差分方法来识别个人税对公司杠杆的影响。以往研究发现2003年减税鼓励了股利派现并降低了权益成本，但并未提供其与均衡杠杆比率的关联。我们估计，减税导致受影响公司的杠杆下降约5个百分点。此外，我们表明，对于边际公司税率较低的公司以及不受融资约束的公司，减税的效应更强，这与我们的理论预测相一致。总体而言，我们发现有力证据表明个人税是公司最优杠杆的一个重要决定因素。
