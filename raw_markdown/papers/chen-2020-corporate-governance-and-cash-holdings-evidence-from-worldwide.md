## Abstract (English)

Using staggered board reforms as a quasi-natural experiment and a difference-in-differences approach, this study examines the impact of corporate governance on cash holdings in 41 countries. We find that board reforms are followed by significant reductions in cash holdings. This effect is more pronounced for firms with weaker pre-reform corporate governance and for firms from countries with weaker institutional environments. Analysis of cash spending suggests that, following board reforms, firms are more likely to use cash to increase R&D expenditures, dividend payouts, and share repurchases, but not to increase capital or acquisition expenditures. Finally, the results indicate that enhanced corporate governance following board reforms leads to higher (lower) cash (dividend payouts) values, consistent with the view that board reforms strengthen corporate governance.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本研究以交错实施的董事会改革作为准自然实验，采用双重差分方法，考察公司治理对41个国家现金持有的影响。我们发现，董事会改革之后现金持有显著减少。这一效应对于改革前公司治理较弱的企业以及来自制度环境较弱国家的企业更为显著。对现金支出的分析表明，在董事会改革之后，企业更可能利用现金来增加研发（R&D）支出、股利支付和股份回购，而非增加资本支出或收购支出。最后，结果表明，董事会改革之后公司治理的增强导致更高（更低）的现金（股利支付）价值，这与董事会改革强化公司治理的观点相一致。
