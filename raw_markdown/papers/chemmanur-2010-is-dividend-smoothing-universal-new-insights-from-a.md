## Abstract (English)

In this paper, we develop new insights about the dynamics of corporate dividend policy by performing the natural experiment of comparing corporate dividend policies in Hong Kong and the U.S., two economies where the tax regime and equity ownership structure are significantly different. Our empirical results can be summarized as follows. First, a test of the Lintner model reveals that the extent of dividend smoothing by firms in Hong Kong is significantly less than those in the U.S. Second, the signaling effects of dividend changes on stock returns are stronger in the U.S. compared to those in Hong Kong. Third, our logit analysis of the determinants of dividend changes indicates that, while the lagged dividend yield significantly affects dividend changes in both countries in the same fashion, prior year stock returns have opposite effects on dividend changes in the two countries. Finally, the extent of dividend smoothing is not systematically related to blockholder equity ownership in either country. Overall, our results suggest that, compared to U.S. firms, Hong Kong firms pursue a more flexible dividend policy commensurate with earnings, and that the differences between the dividend policies of firms in the two countries are consistent with the signaling implications of the differences in the tax regime across the two countries. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

在本文中，我们通过比较香港和美国这两个税收制度和股权所有权结构显著不同的经济体的公司股利政策这一自然实验，对公司股利政策的动态形成了新的认识。我们的实证结果可总结如下。第一，对Lintner模型的检验揭示，香港公司的股利平滑程度显著低于美国公司。第二，股利变动对股票收益的信号效应在美国比在香港更强。第三，我们对股利变动决定因素的logit分析表明，尽管滞后的股利收益率在两个国家以相同方式显著影响股利变动，但前一年的股票收益对两个国家的股利变动具有相反的影响。最后，股利平滑程度在两个国家均与大宗持股人的股权所有权没有系统性关系。总体而言，我们的结果表明，与美国公司相比，香港公司奉行一种与盈余相称、更为灵活的股利政策，且两国公司股利政策之间的差异与两国税收制度差异的信号传递含义相一致。
