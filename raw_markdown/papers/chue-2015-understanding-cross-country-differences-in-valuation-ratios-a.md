## Abstract (English)

We use a variance decomposition approach to examine why aggregate valuation ratios differ across countries. In a cross-section of 22 developed countries from 1980 to 2009, we find that 50 percent of all cross-country differences in the aggregate price-to-book ratio (P/B) can be explained by cross-country differences in expected future five-year profitability. In the second half of our sample period, this percentage exceeds that of the first half, rising to almost 64 percent. Although international differences in accounting standards and conventions may have made earnings from different countries more difficult to compare relative to dividends, we find that it is still cross-country differences in expected future profitability, rather than dividend growth rates, that are more closely related to international differences in valuation ratios. Even among 25 emerging markets, we find that expected future profitability at the five-year horizon can account for 29 percent of all cross-country P/B variations. Our results show that international investors are able to identify substantial cross-country differences in future-earnings prospects and incorporate them into stock market valuations.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们采用方差分解方法，考察总体估值比率为何在国家之间存在差异。在1980至2009年间22个发达国家的横截面中，我们发现总体市账率（P/B）的所有跨国差异中，有50%可由预期未来五年盈利能力的跨国差异来解释。在我们样本期的后半段，这一比例超过了前半段，升至近64%。尽管会计准则和惯例的国际差异可能使不同国家的盈利相比股利更难比较，但我们发现，与国际估值比率差异更密切相关的，仍然是预期未来盈利能力的跨国差异，而非股利增长率。即便在25个新兴市场中，我们也发现五年期预期未来盈利能力可解释所有跨国P/B变动的29%。我们的结果表明，国际投资者能够识别未来盈利前景的重大跨国差异，并将其纳入股票市场估值。
