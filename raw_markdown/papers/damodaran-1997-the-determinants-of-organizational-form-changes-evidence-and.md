## Abstract (English)

We study changes in the real estate industry among organizational forms with varying degrees of restrictiveness and document the associated changes in profitability, free cash flow, debt, dividends, and investment policies. All troubled firms in our sample move to a more flexibile organizational structure, with subsequent reductions in dividends, improvements in performance, and increases in asset sales and investments. Healthy firms that move to a tighter structure have larger free cash flows before the change; they increase dividends, reduce free cash flows and improve profitability after the change. We document evidence of tax considerations in organizational changes.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们研究房地产行业在具有不同限制程度的组织形式之间的变化，并记录了随之而来的盈利能力、自由现金流、债务、股利和投资政策的变化。我们样本中所有陷入困境的公司都转向了更为灵活的组织结构，随后股利减少、业绩改善，资产出售和投资增加。转向更为紧密结构的健康公司在变化之前拥有更大的自由现金流；在变化之后，它们增加股利、减少自由现金流并改善盈利能力。我们记录了组织变化中存在税收考量的证据。
