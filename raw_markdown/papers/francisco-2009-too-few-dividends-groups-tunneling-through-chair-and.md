## Abstract (English)

Group affiliation increases boards' compensation in countries as different as Korea, India, Hong Kong and Italy. In this paper, I examine a 6-year sample of controller-dominated, concentrated-ownership firms in Chile in search of a rationale for these results. I show that, for group-affiliated companies, controllers' presence on the board of directors is associated with a strong negative relation between chair and board compensation and controllers' cash-flow rights. Furthermore, I show that controllers of group-affiliated companies prefer to increase chair and board compensation rather than dividends as their cash-flow rights decrease. (C) 2008 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

在韩国、印度、香港和意大利等差异迥异的国家，集团附属都会提高董事会的薪酬。在本文中，我考察智利一个为期6年的、由控制人主导的、股权集中的公司样本，以探寻这些结果的一种理据。我表明，对于集团附属公司而言，控制人在董事会中的存在与董事长及董事会薪酬与控制人现金流权之间的强烈负向关系相关联。此外，我表明，随着现金流权下降，集团附属公司的控制人更愿意提高董事长及董事会薪酬，而非增加股利。
