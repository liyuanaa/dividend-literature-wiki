## Abstract (English)

This paper measures the value of corporate voting rights, specifically of the control block of votes, in a sample of 661 dual-class firms in 18 countries, in 1997. A consistent measure across countries is proposed. The measure is adjusted for takeover probability, block-holding costs, and dividend and liquidity differences between the share classes. The value of controlblock votes varies widely across countries. It is close to half of firm market value in South Korea, and close to zero in Finland. The value of control-block votes is interpreted as a lower bound for actual private benefits of the controlling shareholder. The legal environment, law enforcement, investor protection, takeover regulations, and power-concentrating corporate charter provisions explain 68% of the cross-country variation in the value of control-block votes. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文测度了公司投票权的价值，特别是控制权投票权区块的价值，样本为1997年18个国家的661家双层股权结构企业。本文提出了一种跨国一致的度量方法。该度量对收购概率、区块持有成本以及不同股份类别之间的股利和流动性差异进行了调整。控制权区块投票权的价值在各国之间差异很大。在韩国，它接近企业市场价值的一半，而在芬兰则接近于零。控制权区块投票权的价值被解释为控股股东实际私人收益的下限。法律环境、法律执行、投资者保护、收购监管以及权力集中的公司章程条款，解释了控制权区块投票权价值跨国差异的68%。
