## Abstract (English)

This study investigates whether bank monitoring influences investor response to a borrowing firm's decision to omit its dividend payments. We establish a new link between the theories of banking and dividend policy in an examination of how bank monitoring and firm dividend signals complement one another to resolve information asymmetries. Results indicate that, for small firms, investors interpret the dividend decision as a function of bank monitoring and the dividend signals taken together. Also reported are the results of tests examining the differences between the monitoring effects of banks versus public and private non-bank lenders. (C) 2001 Elsevier Science B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本研究考察银行监督是否影响投资者对借款企业取消其股利支付这一决定的反应。我们在考察银行监督与企业股利信号如何相互补充以解决信息不对称的过程中，在银行理论与股利政策理论之间建立了一个新的联系。结果表明，对于小企业，投资者将股利决定解读为银行监督与股利信号共同作用的函数。本文还报告了考察银行监督效应与公共及私人非银行贷款人监督效应之间差异的检验结果。
