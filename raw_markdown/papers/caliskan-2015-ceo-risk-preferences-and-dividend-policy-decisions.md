## Abstract (English)

This study examines whether risk aversion-inducing CEO compensation motivates managers to pay more dividends regardless of investor preferences. Using inside debt (i.e., pensions and deferred compensation) and the sensitivity of CEO equity compensation to stock price changes (i.e., high CEO delta), as proxies of CEO risk aversion, we document that inside debt induces CEOs to pay dividends while convex CEO compensation decreases dividend payout (C) 2015 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本研究考察了诱发风险厌恶的CEO薪酬是否促使管理者无论投资者偏好如何都派发更多股利。使用内部债务（即养老金和递延薪酬）以及CEO股权薪酬对股价变化的敏感性（即高CEO delta）作为CEO风险厌恶的代理变量，我们记录到内部债务促使CEO派发股利，而凸性的CEO薪酬则降低股利支付。
