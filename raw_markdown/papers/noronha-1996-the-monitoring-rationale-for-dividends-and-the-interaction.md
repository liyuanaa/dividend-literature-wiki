## Abstract (English)

This paper develops an agency-cost framework for the simultaneous determination of a firm's capital structure and dividend decisions. In the model, simultaneity is contingent on the applicability of Easterbrook's (1984) monitoring rationale for paying dividends, which, in turn, is hypothesized to depend on the existence of alternative sources of monitoring. Estimations of the Rozeff (1982) specification for dividend payout for subsamples stratified according to the prevalence of non-dividend monitoring mechanisms and growth-induced capital market monitoring, confirm the sample-specific validity of the monitoring rationale. A simultaneous system of equations is then estimated and, consistent with our hypothesis, simultaneity between capital structure and dividend decisions is observed only for the subsample in which the monitoring rationale for dividends is found applicable.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文建立了一个代理成本框架，用于同时决定企业的资本结构和股利决策。在该模型中，同时性取决于伊斯特布鲁克（Easterbrook，1984）支付股利的监督理由的适用性，而这一理由被假设取决于是否存在替代性监督来源。针对根据非股利监督机制和成长引致的资本市场监督的普遍程度所分层的子样本，对罗泽夫（Rozeff，1982）股利支付设定的估计，确认了该监督理由在特定样本中的有效性。随后估计了一个联立方程组，与我们的假设相一致，只有在监督理由被发现适用的子样本中，才观察到资本结构与股利决策之间的同时性。
