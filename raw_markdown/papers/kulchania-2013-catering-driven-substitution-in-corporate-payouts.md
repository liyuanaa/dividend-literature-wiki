## Abstract (English)

This paper investigates catering as a motivation for substitution between share repurchases and dividend payments. I hypothesize that firms cater to investor demand by repurchasing shares when investors place a premium on the stock price of firms that repurchase shares, and by paying dividends when investors place a higher value on dividend-paying firms. I propose a proxy to measure the relative preference for repurchases over dividends the difference premium. Results show that the decision to repurchase shares or to pay dividends depends on this premium. Firms channel higher fractions of the additional payout dollars toward share repurchases when this premium is high. The market reaction to dividend changes is more favorable when firms act in accordance with the catering hypothesis. Overall, I find that catering plays a role in the substitution between repurchases and dividends. (C) 2013 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文研究迎合作为股份回购与股利派发之间替代的一种动机。我假设企业通过在投资者对回购股份企业的股价给予溢价时回购股份、以及在投资者对派发股利企业给予更高估值时派发股利，来迎合投资者需求。我提出一个代理变量来度量回购相对于股利的相对偏好——差异溢价（difference premium）。结果表明，回购股份还是派发股利的决策取决于这一溢价。当该溢价较高时，企业将更高比例的额外派现资金投向股份回购。当企业按照迎合假说行事时，市场对股利变化的反应更为有利。总体而言，我发现迎合在回购与股利之间的替代中发挥了作用。
