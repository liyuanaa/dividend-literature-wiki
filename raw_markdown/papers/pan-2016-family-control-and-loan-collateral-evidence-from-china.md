## Abstract (English)

Using a sample of 612 listed Chinese non-SOEs from 2006 to 2009, we show that the use of collateral is higher in family-controlled firms. This effect is more pronounced when family firms have a larger control ownership wedge, family representation in management, and are led by a descendant chairman/CEO. We further document that having multiple large shareholders, paying higher dividends, having completed the split-share structure reform and being located in provinces with more competitive credit markets can mitigate the incentive of controlling families to engage in expropriation and reduce the level of collateral required. Overall, we contend that in China, the risk of expropriation associated with family control leads to an increased credit risk and, in turn, higher collateral being required by banks. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

使用2006至2009年间612家中国上市非国有企业的样本，我们表明抵押品的使用在家族控制企业中更高。当家族企业拥有更大的控制权与所有权楔子（control ownership wedge）、家族在管理层中有代表、并由后代董事长/CEO领导时，这一效应更为显著。我们进一步记录到，拥有多个大股东、派发更高股利、已完成股权分置改革以及位于信贷市场更具竞争性省份的企业，可以缓解控制性家族进行侵占的动机，并降低所需抵押品的水平。总体而言，我们认为在中国，与家族控制相关的侵占风险导致信用风险增加，进而导致银行要求更高的抵押品。
