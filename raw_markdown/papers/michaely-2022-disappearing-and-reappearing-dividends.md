## Abstract (English)

We decompose the decrease (1970s-20 0 0) and subsequent recovery (20 0 0-2018) in the fraction of dividend-paying firms. Changes in firm characteristics and proclivity to pay (probability of paying dividends conditional on characteristics) each drive half of the dividend disappearance. A higher proclivity drives 82% of the dividend reappearance. The remaining 18% is driven by a single characteristic: reduced earnings volatility. Changing characteristics are associated with low-profitability, high-earnings-volatility firms. Changing proclivity is associated with stable, profitable firms. Rather than dividend initiations or omissions, newly listed and delisted firms drive trends. Finally, the magnitude and duration of disappearing total payout is substantially smaller than that of dividends, indicating some substitution between dividends and repurchases. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们分解了派发股利企业比例的下降（1970年代至2000年）及随后的回升（2000至2018年）。企业特征的变化以及支付倾向（在给定特征条件下派发股利的概率）各自驱动了股利消失的一半。更高的支付倾向驱动了股利重现的82%。剩余的18%由单一特征驱动：盈余波动性的降低。变化的特征与低盈利能力、高盈余波动性的企业相关联。变化的支付倾向与稳定、盈利的企业相关联。驱动趋势的并非股利启动或取消，而是新上市和退市的企业。最后，消失的总支付在量级和持续时间上都大大小于股利，这表明股利与回购之间存在某种替代。
