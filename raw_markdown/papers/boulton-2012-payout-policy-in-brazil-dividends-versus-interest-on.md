## Abstract (English)

We examine the impact of tax burden on cash distribution using a sample of Brazilian firms, which are allowed by law to distribute cash to shareholders in two forms: dividends and tax-advantaged interest on equity. The Brazilian institutional setting is superior to those used in prior studies that examine the choice between dividends and capital gains because, in some cases, dividends provide advantages that outweigh their negative tax consequences, leading firms to rationally choose payout policies that are not optimal when viewed only from the perspective of taxes. We find that taxes are a primary determinant of Brazilian firms' payout policy decisions, as profitability and payout ratios (nonequity tax shields) are positively (negatively) related to the likelihood that a firm pays interest on equity. However, many firms continue to pay dividends despite the tax advantages of interest on equity payments. Abnormal returns around payout policy announcements suggest that these firms are, at least in part, catering to investor demand. (C) 2011 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们使用巴西企业样本，考察税负对现金分配的影响，巴西法律允许企业以两种形式向股东分配现金：股利和享有税收优惠的股本利息（interest on equity）。巴西的制度环境优于以往研究中考察股利与资本利得之间选择的环境，因为在某些情况下，股利提供的优势超过其负面的税收后果，从而导致企业理性地选择那些仅从税收角度看并非最优的支付政策。我们发现，税收是巴西企业支付政策决策的主要决定因素，因为盈利能力和支付率（非股本税盾）与企业支付股本利息的可能性正（负）相关。然而，尽管股本利息支付具有税收优势，许多企业仍继续支付股利。支付政策公告前后的异常回报表明，这些企业至少在一定程度上是在迎合投资者需求。
