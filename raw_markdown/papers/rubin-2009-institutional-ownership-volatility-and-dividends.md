## Abstract (English)

We find that the sign of the correlation between institutional ownership and volatility depends on the firm's dividend policy: institutional ownership is negatively (positively) related to volatility among non-dividend (dividend) paying stocks. The empirical results are consistent with an interaction between institutional preference for low volatility and the tendency of higher levels of institutional ownership to increase volatility through their trading behavior. This result is robust to many control variables and possible endogeneity concerns. Supporting our conjecture that institutions herd on dividend signals we find that the correlation between turnover and institutional ownership is higher for dividend paying stocks, and that the positive correlation between turnover and institutional ownership is higher on dividend declaration days. Finally, we also find that the level of institutional ownership drops following an increase in volatility for both dividend payers and non-payers, and that volatility rises following increased institutional ownership for dividend paying stocks. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们发现，机构持股与波动率之间相关性的符号取决于企业的股利政策：在不派发股利（派发股利）的股票中，机构持股与波动率负相关（正相关）。实证结果与以下相互作用相一致：机构对低波动率的偏好，与更高水平的机构持股通过其交易行为增加波动率的倾向之间的相互作用。这一结果对众多控制变量和可能的内生性问题都是稳健的。为支持我们关于机构在股利信号上羊群行为的猜想，我们发现换手率与机构持股之间的相关性在派发股利的股票中更高，且换手率与机构持股之间的正相关在股利宣告日更高。最后，我们还发现，对于股利派发者和非派发者，机构持股水平在波动率上升后都会下降，且对于派发股利的股票，波动率在机构持股增加后会上升。
