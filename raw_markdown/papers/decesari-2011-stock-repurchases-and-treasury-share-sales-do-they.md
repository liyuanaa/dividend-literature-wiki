## Abstract (English)

Can companies reduce the volatility and increase the liquidity of their stocks by trading them? In the context of the Italian stock market, where companies have far more leeway to sell as well as buy their own stocks than in the U.S., the answer is yes. We examine the effects of trading (open-market share repurchases and treasury shares sales) on liquidity (bid-ask spread) and volatility (return variance). Further, we examine the impact of shareholder approvals of repurchase programs on liquidity and volatility. We find clear evidence that trading increases liquidity and reduces volatility. These results are consistent with our analysis of the motives Italian companies give for making share repurchases. (C) 2011 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

公司能否通过交易自身股票来降低其股价波动性并提高流动性？在意大利股票市场的背景下——那里公司在出售和买入自身股票方面比在美国拥有更大的回旋余地——答案是肯定的。我们检验了交易（公开市场股份回购和库存股出售）对流动性（买卖价差）和波动性（收益方差）的影响。此外，我们检验了股东批准回购计划对流动性和波动性的影响。我们发现明确的证据表明交易增加了流动性并降低了波动性。这些结果与我们对意大利公司进行股份回购所给出动机的分析相一致。
