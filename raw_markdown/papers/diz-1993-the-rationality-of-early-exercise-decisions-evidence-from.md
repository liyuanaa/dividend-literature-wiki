## Abstract (English)

This study provides a comprehensive empirical analysis of the early exercise history of S&P 100 put and call options. Even though the S&P 100 index options market is generally considered to be the most efficient options market in the world, we show that many exercise decisions are inefficient because they occur when recorded bids are greater than exercise values. Due to market imperfections, some of the cases of inefficient exercise are still rational, but we show that a substantial number of these decisions are clearly irrational, since it would have been possible to realize a larger riskless net cash flow by selling. Unlike previous studies of early exercise, our tests of efficiency and the rational decisions that presumably lead to efficient markets are model independent. We also provide evidence concerning the relative significance of dividends and the wild card to index option pricing models, and introduce and document the importance of the option to exercise and avoid the indirect costs of the spread. We also find evidence of a significant day-of-the-week exercise effect, and present some likely explanations for that effect.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本研究对标准普尔100看跌期权和看涨期权的提前行权历史进行了全面的实证分析。尽管标准普尔100指数期权市场通常被认为是世界上最有效的期权市场，我们表明许多行权决策是低效的，因为它们发生在记录的买价大于行权价值之时。由于市场不完美，一些低效行权的情形仍然是理性的，但我们表明这些决策中有相当一部分显然是非理性的，因为通过卖出本可以实现更大的无风险净现金流。与此前关于提前行权的研究不同，我们对效率和据称导致有效市场的理性决策的检验是与模型无关的。我们还提供了关于股利和“通配符”对指数期权定价模型相对重要性的证据，并引入并记录了行权以避免价差间接成本这一选择的重要性。我们还发现存在显著的一周中不同日的行权效应，并对该效应给出了一些可能的解释。
