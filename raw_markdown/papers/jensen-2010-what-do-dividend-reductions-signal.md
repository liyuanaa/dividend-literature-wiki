## Abstract (English)

Dividend reductions have long been considered a "last resort" action for firm managers. Managerial reluctance to reduce dividends emanates from the view that dividend drops signal managerial pessimism regarding future earnings. Contrary to expectations, studies show that earnings rebound significantly following a dividend reduction; yet investors react negatively to the dividend-drop announcement. We present an explanation for the anomalous behavior of earnings and returns around the time of a dividend drop. Our evidence suggests that a reduction in a firm's established dividend coincides with a decrease in the value of the firm's real options. Earnings rebound following the dividend reduction due to the savings that result as the firm allows growth options to expire; however, announcement period returns suggest that investors recognize the lost value associated with the forthcoming expiration of growth options. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

长期以来，股利削减一直被视为公司管理者的“最后手段”行动。管理者不愿削减股利源于这样一种观点：股利下降会发出管理者对未来盈余持悲观态度的信号。与预期相反，研究表明盈余在股利削减后会显著反弹；然而投资者对股利下降公告作出负向反应。我们为股利下降前后盈余和收益的这种反常行为提出了一个解释。我们的证据表明，公司既定股利的削减与该公司实物期权价值的下降相吻合。盈余在股利削减后反弹，这是由于公司允许增长期权到期而产生的节约；然而，公告期收益表明投资者认识到与即将到来的增长期权到期相关的价值损失。
