## Abstract (English)

Following the dividend flexibility hypothesis used by DeAngelo and DeAngelo (2006), Blau and Fuller (2008), and others, we theoretically extend the proposition of DeAngelo and DeAngelo (2006) optimal payout policy in terms of the flexibility dividend hypothesis. In addition, we also introduce growth rate, systematic risk, and total risk variables into the theoretical model. To test the theoretical results derived in this paper, we use the data collected in the US from 1969 to 2009 to investigate the impact of the growth rate, systematic risk, and total risk on the optimal payout ratio in terms of the fixed-effect model. We find that based on flexibility considerations, a company will reduce its payout when the growth rate increases. In addition, we find that a nonlinear relationship exists between the payout ratio and the risk. In other words, the relationship between the payout ratio and the risk is negative (or positive) when the growth rate is higher (or lower) than the rate of return on total assets. Our theoretical model and empirical results can therefore be used to identify whether flexibility or the free cash flow hypothesis should be used to determine the dividend policy. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

遵循迪安杰洛和迪安杰洛（DeAngelo and DeAngelo，2006）、布劳和富勒（Blau and Fuller，2008）等人使用的股利灵活性假说，我们从灵活性股利假说的角度，从理论上扩展了迪安杰洛和迪安杰洛（2006）的最优支付政策命题。此外，我们还将成长率、系统性风险和总风险变量引入理论模型。为检验本文推导的理论结果，我们使用1969年至2009年间在美国收集的数据，采用固定效应模型考察成长率、系统性风险和总风险对最优支付率的影响。我们发现，基于灵活性考量，当成长率上升时，公司会减少其支付。此外，我们发现支付率与风险之间存在非线性关系。换言之，当成长率高于（或低于）总资产回报率时，支付率与风险之间的关系为负（或正）。因此，我们的理论模型和实证结果可用于识别应使用灵活性假说还是自由现金流假说来决定股利政策。
