## Abstract (English)

Recent theoretical models suggest debt and dividends can serve as substitute free cash how control or signaling devices. I examine share price responses to announcements of straight debt issues and test whether there are systematic differences between low and high dividend payout firms. Share price response is significantly positive for low growth-low dividend payout firms, and is negatively related to cross-sectional dividend payout. The results support arguments that debt and dividends are substitutes. The results also support arguments that debt provides free cash flow or signaling benefits, but suggest the benefits are significant only for firms with low levels of substitutes. I also document that low growth-low dividend payout firms enter capital markets less frequently, but find no relation between share price response and this frequency.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

近来的理论模型表明，债务和股利可以充当替代性的自由现金流控制或信号工具。我考察了股价对普通债务发行公告的反应，并检验低股利支付企业与高股利支付企业之间是否存在系统性差异。对于低成长-低股利支付企业，股价反应显著为正，且与横截面股利支付负相关。这些结果支持了债务与股利互为替代品的论点。结果还支持了债务提供自由现金流或信号收益的论点，但表明这些收益只对替代品水平较低的企业才显著。我还记录到，低成长-低股利支付企业进入资本市场的频率较低，但未发现股价反应与这一频率之间存在关系。
