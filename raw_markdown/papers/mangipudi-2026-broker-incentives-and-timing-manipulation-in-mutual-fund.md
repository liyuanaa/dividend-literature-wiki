## Abstract (English)

This paper examines how financial intermediaries, particularly brokers, influence the timing of retail investor flows into mutual funds at the turn of the calendar year. Using mutual fund purchase (inflows) and redemption (outflows) data, we document a systematic shift in inflows from January to December in broker-sold funds, consistent with brokers accelerating purchases to meet year-end sales quotas under non-linear compensation structures. This timing distortion is absent in direct-sold funds and weakens following the implementation of Regulation Best Interest, which reduces brokers' incentives to manipulate investment timing. Our findings are robust to alternative explanations based on capital gains or dividend distributions, fund marketing efforts, or investor behaviors such as year-end liquidity planning and procrastination. These findings extend theories of sales-driven behavior to financial intermediation and highlight a new dimension of agency conflict.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文考察金融中介、尤其是经纪商，如何在日历年之交影响散户投资者流入共同基金的时机。使用共同基金申购（流入）和赎回（流出）数据，我们记录到经纪商销售基金的流入从1月到12月出现系统性转移，这与经纪商在非线性薪酬结构下为达成年终销售配额而加速申购相一致。这种时机扭曲在直销基金中不存在，并在实施《最佳利益监管条例》（Regulation Best Interest）之后减弱，该条例降低了经纪商操纵投资时机的动机。我们的发现对基于资本利得或股利分配、基金营销努力，或诸如年终流动性规划和拖延等投资者行为的替代解释都稳健。这些发现将销售驱动行为的理论拓展到金融中介领域，并凸显了代理冲突的一个新维度。
