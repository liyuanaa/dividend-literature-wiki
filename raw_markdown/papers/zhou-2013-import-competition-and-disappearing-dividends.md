## Abstract (English)

Import competition has had an enormous impact on the US economy. This paper looks at whether such competition has increased the risk of US firms and is a factor that can partly explain the Fama-French disappearing dividends phenomenon. We show that firms facing more import competition do face more uncertainty in their future performance, and are less likely to pay a dividend. Import penetration through a risk channel has significant explanatory power, even after we include a variety of controls suggested in the literature, as well as Herfindahl index that controls for market power. Further, we can explain a significant part of the disappearing dividends phenomenon. Finally, we also find evidence that import competition contributes to the substitution of share repurchases for cash dividends. We are the first to document the impact that increased import competition and "globalization" have had on the dividend policy of US firms. Journal of International Business Studies (2013) 44, 138-154. doi: 10.1057/jibs.2012.36

## 摘要（中文翻译）

进口竞争对美国经济产生了巨大影响。本文考察这种竞争是否增加了美国企业的风险，以及它是否是能够部分解释法玛-弗伦奇（Fama-French）股利消失现象的一个因素。我们表明，面临更激烈进口竞争的企业在其未来业绩上确实面临更多不确定性，且更不可能支付股利。进口渗透通过风险渠道具有显著的解释力，即使在我们纳入了文献中建议的各种控制变量以及控制市场势力的赫芬达尔指数（Herfindahl index）之后依然如此。此外，我们能够解释股利消失现象的很大一部分。最后，我们还发现证据表明，进口竞争促成了用股份回购替代现金股利。我们首次记录了进口竞争加剧和"全球化"对美国企业股利政策所产生的影响。《国际商务研究杂志》（2013）44, 138-154。doi: 10.1057/jibs.2012.36
