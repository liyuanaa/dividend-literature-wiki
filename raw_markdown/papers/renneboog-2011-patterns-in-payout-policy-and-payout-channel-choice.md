## Abstract (English)

We study the decision to distribute funds as well as the choice of the payout channel (i.e. dividends, repurchases, or both). Our analysis of the payout policy of UK firms demonstrates that the importance of share repurchases is increasing, but dividends still constitute a vast proportion of the total payout. We document that there is a relation between the presence of blockholders and the choice of the payout channel. We find that payout decisions are influenced by directors' liquidity needs but are not consistent with the agency theory of payout. We also reject the tax-clientele explanation for payout choices. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们研究分配资金的决策以及支付渠道（即股利、回购或两者兼有）的选择。我们对英国企业支付政策的分析表明，股份回购的重要性正在上升，但股利仍占总支付的绝大部分。我们记录到，大股东的存在与支付渠道的选择之间存在关系。我们发现，支付决策受董事流动性需求的影响，但与支付的代理理论不一致。我们还否定了支付选择的税收群体解释。
