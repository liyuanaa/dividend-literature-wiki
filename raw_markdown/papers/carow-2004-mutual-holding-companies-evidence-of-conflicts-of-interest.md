## Abstract (English)

The mutual holding company (MHC) structure establishes a dual-class stock that creates a unique opportunity to transfer wealth from thrift depositor-owners to new minority shareholders through the disparate payment of dividends. We show that MHCs are priced higher than comparable non-MHCs and dividend policy is a significant component of this valuation. We also show that MHC thrifts pay significantly higher dividends than non-MHC thrifts and that an Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS) ruling reducing the potential for disparate dividends between the two classes of shareholders resulted in lower dividends. These results have policy implications of special significance given that the OTS reversed its position in 2000 and because of the current controversy over the use of the MHC structure in the financial service industry. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

互助控股公司（MHC）结构设立了一种双重股权类别，这创造了一个独特的机会，可以通过差别化的股利支付将财富从储蓄机构的存款人—所有者转移给新的少数股东。我们表明，MHC的定价高于可比的非MHC，且股利政策是这一估值的一个重要组成部分。我们还表明，MHC储蓄机构支付的股利显著高于非MHC储蓄机构，并且储蓄机构监理局（OTS）一项旨在降低两类股东之间出现差别化股利可能性的裁定导致了股利下降。鉴于OTS在2000年逆转了其立场，以及当前围绕金融服务业中使用MHC结构的争议，这些结果具有特别重要的政策意义。
