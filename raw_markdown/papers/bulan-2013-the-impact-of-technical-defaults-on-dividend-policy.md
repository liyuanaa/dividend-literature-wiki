## Abstract (English)

This paper examines how loan covenant violations impact firm dividend policy. Using contract-level loan data for nonfinancial firms in the US, this study provides evidence that the occurrence of a covenant violation significantly increases the likelihood of a dividend reduction in the subsequent quarter. Moreover, we show that the degree of creditor-shareholder conflict and firm financial constraints are important determinants of dividend cuts upon technical default. Additionally, this paper finds the tendency of dividend cuts upon technical default weakened after the repeal of the Glass-Steagall Act. These findings suggest that loan covenants serve a critical role in mitigating creditor-shareholder conflicts. (C) 2012 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文考察了贷款契约违约如何影响公司股利政策。使用美国非金融公司的合同层面贷款数据，本研究提供的证据表明，契约违约的发生显著增加了随后一个季度削减股利的可能性。此外，我们表明债权人-股东冲突的程度和公司融资约束是技术性违约时削减股利的重要决定因素。另外，本文发现技术性违约时削减股利的倾向在《格拉斯-斯蒂格尔法案》废除后减弱了。这些发现表明贷款契约在缓解债权人-股东冲突中发挥着关键作用。
