## Abstract (English)

Considerable evidence exists to support the hypothesis that the payment of dividends provides information that helps investors and analysts value the firm. We find that dividend omissions precede increases in return variance, beta and the dispersion of analyst forecasts of earnings. However, the variance of actual earnings also increases after dividend omissions. An increase in the variance of earnings implies a decrease in the predictability of earnings even if there is no change in information availability. The evidence is consistent with the hypothesis that managers omit dividends because earnings become inherently less predictable. In other words, managers use dividends as a signaling mechanism. We also report a negative association between stock-price reactions to omission announcements and changes in beta, but not changes in total return variance or earnings variance, consistent with increases in priced estimation risk.

## 摘要（中文翻译）

存在大量证据支持如下假说，即股利的支付提供了有助于投资者和分析师对企业进行估值的信息。我们发现股利取消先于收益方差、贝塔（beta）以及分析师盈利预测离散度的增加。然而，实际盈利的方差在股利取消后也会增加。盈利方差的增加意味着盈利可预测性的下降，即使信息可得性没有变化。该证据与如下假说一致，即管理者取消股利是因为盈利本质上变得不那么可预测。换言之，管理者将股利用作一种信号传递机制。我们还报告了对取消公告的股价反应与贝塔变化之间存在负向关联，但与总收益方差或盈利方差的变化则不然，这与被计价的估计风险（priced estimation risk）的增加相一致。
