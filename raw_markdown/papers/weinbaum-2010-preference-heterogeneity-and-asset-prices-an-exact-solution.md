## Abstract (English)

We introduce a general equilibrium model of a multi-agent, pure-exchange economy and find a set of conditions that enable us to obtain explicit closed-form solutions to the equilibrium interest rate, stock price, risk premium and stock market volatility when investors have heterogenous risk aversions. Because the market is dynamically complete, full risk sharing obtains and a representative agent can be constructed, though the risk aversion of this agent fluctuates over time with the state of the economy, as the relative wealth distribution of the individual investors changes. We show that preference heterogeneity can cause asset prices to be significantly more volatile than the underlying dividends and that it can lead to leverage-like effects in volatility, in the sense that volatility increases after stock-market declines. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们引入了一个关于多主体纯交换经济体的一般均衡模型，并找到一组条件，使我们能够在投资者具有异质风险规避时，获得均衡利率、股票价格、风险溢价和股票市场波动性的显式闭式解。由于市场是动态完全的，可以实现充分的风险分担，并可以构建一个代表性主体，尽管随着经济状态的变化，随着各个投资者相对财富分布的改变，该主体的风险规避会随时间波动。我们表明，偏好异质性能够使资产价格的波动性显著大于基础股利的波动性，并且它能够导致波动性中出现类似杠杆的效应，即波动性在股市下跌之后上升。
