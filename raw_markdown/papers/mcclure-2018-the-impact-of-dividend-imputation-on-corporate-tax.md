## Abstract (English)

The objective of this paper is to evaluate whether dividend imputation, whereby tax credits may be passed on to shareholders for corporate tax paid, impacts corporate tax avoidance. This is undertaken with a pooled cross-sectional research design evaluating differences in tax avoidance across firms where there are significant differences in corporate tax avoidance incentives. Specifically, potential differences arise between firms paying dividends with tax credits, paying dividends without tax credits, and not paying dividends. Results suggest that firms paying dividends with tax credits attached are less likely to engage in tax avoidance with an average cash effective tax rate up to 16.9 percentage points higher than firms that pay dividends without tax credits, and up to 14.7 percentage points higher than firms that do not pay dividends at all. Accordingly, this provides insights into the effectiveness of dividend imputation in mitigating corporate tax avoidance, as well as providing support for the continuance of dividend imputation in Australia. Additionally, a positive association is found to exist between outside directors and corporate tax avoidance, extending to firms paying dividends with tax credits where dividend imputation is expected to mitigate such a relation. In combination, these results suggest heterogeneity of costs and benefits of tax avoidance and this is a challenge in evaluating corporate tax aggressiveness generally, and the impact of corporate governance on corporate tax avoidance in particular. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

本文的目的是评估股利抵免制度（据此可将企业已缴纳的公司税以税收抵免形式传递给股东）是否会影响企业避税。这一研究采用混合横截面研究设计，评估在企业避税激励存在显著差异的公司之间避税行为的差异。具体而言，潜在差异存在于以下三类公司之间：支付附带税收抵免股利的公司、支付不附带税收抵免股利的公司，以及不支付股利的公司。结果表明，支付附带税收抵免股利的公司更不太可能从事避税，其平均现金实际税率比支付不附带税收抵免股利的公司最多高出16.9个百分点，比完全不支付股利的公司最多高出14.7个百分点。据此，这为股利抵免制度在抑制企业避税方面的有效性提供了洞见，同时也为澳大利亚继续实施股利抵免制度提供了支持。此外，研究发现外部董事与企业避税之间存在正相关关系，这种关系延伸至那些支付附带税收抵免股利的公司，而股利抵免制度本应缓解此种关系。综合来看，这些结果表明避税的成本与收益具有异质性，这对于总体上评估企业税收激进程度，尤其是评估公司治理对企业避税的影响，构成了一项挑战。
