## Abstract (English)

We examine the effects of executive compensation and investor protection on payout policy in Europe. We find a negative (positive) relationship between both option and restricted stock compensation and dividends (repurchases). However, when the incentive compensation is dividend protected, dividend payouts increase. Firms in weak investor protection countries pay higher dividends consistent with maintaining a reputation for distributing excess free cash flows. However, growth firms in weak investor protection countries reduce dividends (increase repurchases) in relation to increases in equity-incentive compensation. Our results are consistent with growth firms in weak investor protection countries using equity incentives as a substitute for dividends to reduce agency costs. (C) 2014 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文翻译）

我们考察高管薪酬和投资者保护对欧洲支付政策的影响。我们发现，期权薪酬和限制性股票薪酬与股利（回购）之间存在负（正）相关关系。然而，当激励薪酬受到股利保护时，股利支付会增加。投资者保护薄弱国家的企业支付更高的股利，这与维护分配多余自由现金流的声誉相一致。然而，投资者保护薄弱国家的成长型企业，其股利减少（回购增加）与股权激励薪酬的增加相关。我们的结果与以下观点相一致：投资者保护薄弱国家的成长型企业使用股权激励作为股利的替代品，以降低代理成本。
