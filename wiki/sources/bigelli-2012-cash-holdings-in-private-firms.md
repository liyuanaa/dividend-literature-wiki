## Abstract

Evidence from a wide sample of Italian private firms shows that cash holdings are significantly related with smaller size, higher risk and lower effective tax rates, therefore supporting predictions from the trade-off model. More cash is also held by firms with longer cash conversion cycles and lower financing deficits, as predicted by the financing hierarchy theory. Reported evidence also shows that dividend payments are associated with more cash holdings, and both bank debt and net working capital represent good cash-substitutes. When controlling for macroeconomic and industry factors, some variables lose their significance, but the general findings are confirmed. Finally, cash-rich companies are found to be more profitable, to pay more dividends and to invest more in a medium-term future horizon. (C) 2011 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

来自意大利大量私营公司样本的证据表明，现金持有与较小的规模、较高的风险和较低的有效税率显著相关，从而支持了权衡模型（trade-off model）的预测。现金转换周期较长、融资赤字较低的公司也持有更多现金，正如融资等级理论（financing hierarchy theory）所预测。所报告的证据还表明，股利支付与更多的现金持有相关联，而银行债务和净营运资本都是良好的现金替代品。在控制宏观经济和行业因素时，一些变量丧失了显著性，但总体发现得到了证实。最后，现金充裕的公司被发现盈利能力更强、支付更多股利，并在中期未来视野内进行更多投资。
