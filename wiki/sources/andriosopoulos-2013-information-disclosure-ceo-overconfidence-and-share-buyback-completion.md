## Abstract

An open market share buyback is not a firm commitment, and there is limited evidence on whether firms repurchase the intended shares. Unlike US studies, we use data from unique UK regulatory and disclosure environment that allows to accurately measure the share buyback completion rates. We show that information disclosure and CEO overconfidence are significant determinants of the share buyback completion rate. In addition, we find that large and widely held firms that conduct subsequent buyback programs and have a past buyback completion reputation exhibit higher completion rates. Finally, we assess whether other CEO characteristics affect buyback completion rates and find that firms with senior CEOs who hold external directorships and have a longer tenure as CEO are more likely to complete the buyback programs. In sum, our results suggest there is a clear relationship between information disclosure, CEO overconfidence, and buyback completion rates. (C) 2013 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

公开市场股票回购并非一项确定性承诺，关于公司是否会回购其计划回购的股份，现有证据有限。与美国的研究不同，我们使用来自英国独特监管和披露环境的数据，从而能够准确衡量股票回购完成率。我们表明，信息披露和CEO过度自信是股票回购完成率的重要决定因素。此外，我们发现，规模大、股权分散、且开展后续回购计划并具有既往回购完成声誉的公司表现出更高的完成率。最后，我们评估了其他CEO特征是否影响回购完成率，发现拥有资深CEO（其持有外部董事职位且任CEO时间较长）的公司更可能完成回购计划。总之，我们的结果表明，信息披露、CEO过度自信与回购完成率之间存在明确的关系。
