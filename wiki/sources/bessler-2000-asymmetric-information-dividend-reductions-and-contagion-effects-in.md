## Abstract

In an environment of asymmetric information, banks face information externalities due to their role as intermediaries of information. In particular, bank insiders will possess private information from monitoring loan customers. Accordingly, outsiders may interpret changes in a bank's financial policy as signals about the quality of its loan portfolio and to the extent that the assets (loans) of different banks are viewed as similar, they will interpret such signals as pertaining to non-announcing banks as well leading to contagion effects. We test for the presence of contagion effects in stock returns associated with announcements of dividend cuts by money-center banks. We find that dividend cuts induce negative abnormal returns in the stocks of non-announcing money-center banks and to a lesser extent in the stocks of large regional banks. The observed contagion effects appear consistent with informed rather than contagious panic behavior because these effects are systematically related to risks that are common to all affected banks. ## 摘要（中文）

在非对称信息的环境中，银行由于其作为信息中介的角色而面临信息外部性。特别是，银行内部人将从监督贷款客户中获得私人信息。因此，外部人可能将银行财务政策的变化解读为关于其贷款组合质量的信号，且在不同银行的资产（贷款）被视为相似的程度上，他们会将此类信号解读为也适用于未发布公告的银行，从而导致传染效应。我们检验了与货币中心银行削减股利公告相关联的股票收益中传染效应的存在。我们发现，削减股利在未发布公告的货币中心银行股票中引起负向异常收益，并在较小程度上在大型区域性银行股票中引起负向异常收益。观察到的传染效应似乎与知情行为而非传染性恐慌行为相一致，因为这些效应与所有受影响银行共同面临的风险系统性相关。
