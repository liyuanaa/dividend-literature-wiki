## Abstract

This study identifies a J-shaped relation between dividends and firm value. On average, top dividend-payers are valued higher than all other firms, while non-dividend-payers are valued higher than low-dividend-payers. This J-shaped relation is highly stable over time as it is observed in nearly every year over the period 1962-2010, and it remains significant after controlling for firm characteristics such as profitability, growth and firm size. We also find similar J-shaped relations in stock markets outside the U.S. Our evidence suggests that dividend theories, such as the dividend catering, free-cash-flow and dividend clientele hypotheses, are inadequate to explain the J-shaped relation. It does not appear that the J-shaped pattern reflects mispricing. There is mixed evidence as to whether the J-shaped relation is driven by dividends or unobservable firm characteristics. ## 摘要（中文）

本研究识别出股利与公司价值之间存在J形关系。平均而言，最高股利支付者的估值高于所有其他公司，而不派息公司的估值高于低股利支付者。这种J形关系在时间上高度稳定，因为在1962-2010年期间几乎每一年都能观察到，并且在控制了盈利能力、增长和公司规模等公司特征之后仍然显著。我们还在美国以外的股票市场中发现了类似的J形关系。我们的证据表明，诸如股利迎合、自由现金流和股利客户群假说等股利理论不足以解释这种J形关系。这种J形模式似乎并非反映错误定价。关于该J形关系是由股利还是由不可观测的公司特征驱动，证据是混合的。
