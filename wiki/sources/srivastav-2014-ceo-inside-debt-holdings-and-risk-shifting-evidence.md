## Abstract

Bank payouts divert cash to shareholders, while leaving behind riskier and less liquid assets to repay debt holders in the future. Bank payouts, therefore, constitute a type of risk-shifting that benefits equity holders at the expense of debt holders. In this paper, we provide insights on how CEO incentives stemming from inside debt (primarily defined benefit pensions and deferred compensation) impact bank payout policy in a manner that protects debt holder interests. We show that CEOs with higher inside debt relative to inside equity are associated with more conservative bank payout policies. Specifically, CEOs paid with more inside debt are more likely to cut payouts and to cut payouts by a larger amount. Reductions in payouts occur through a decrease in both dividends and repurchases. Our results also hold over a subsample of TARP banks where we expect the link between risk-shifting and payouts to be of particular relevance because it involves wealth transfers from the taxpayer to equity holders. We conclude that inside debt can help in addressing risk-shifting concerns by aligning the interests of CEOs with those of creditors, regulators, and in the case of TARP banks, the taxpayer. ## 摘要（中文）

银行派现将现金转移给股东，同时留下风险更高、流动性更差的资产以在未来偿还债权人。因此，银行派现构成了一种风险转移，它以损害债权人利益为代价使股权持有人受益。在本文中，我们提供了关于源于内部债务（主要是固定收益养老金和递延薪酬）的CEO激励如何以保护债权人利益的方式影响银行派现政策的见解。我们表明，内部债务相对于内部股权较高的CEO与更保守的银行派现政策相关。具体而言，以更多内部债务支付薪酬的CEO更有可能削减派现，且削减的幅度更大。派现的减少通过股利和回购的双重减少来实现。我们的结果在TARP银行子样本中也成立，在这一子样本中，我们预期风险转移与派现之间的联系尤为相关，因为它涉及从纳税人到股权持有人的财富转移。我们得出结论，内部债务能够通过使CEO的利益与债权人、监管者、以及在TARP银行情形下与纳税人的利益相一致，从而有助于解决风险转移担忧。
