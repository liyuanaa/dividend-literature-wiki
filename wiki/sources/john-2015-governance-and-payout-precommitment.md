## Abstract

We examine how firms structure payout and debt commitments to address governance weaknesses. Firms with severe agency conflicts precommit through a combination of dividends and debt or through dividends rather than debt alone. Such firms also shift their shareholder payouts towards regular quarterly dividends a stronger commitment than special dividends or repurchases. Although dividend commitments are implicit, event study evidence supports their credibility and value relevance for firms with weak governance. Despite harsher penalties, debt alone cannot replace shareholder payouts as a means of addressing managerial agency conflicts. Published by Elsevier B.V.

## 摘要（中文）

我们考察企业如何构建支付和债务承诺以应对治理弱点。存在严重代理冲突的企业会通过股利与债务的组合，或通过股利而非单独的债务来进行预先承诺。此类企业还会将其股东支付转向常规季度股利——这比特别股利或回购是更强的承诺。尽管股利承诺是隐性的，但事件研究的证据支持其对治理薄弱企业的可信度和价值相关性。尽管惩罚更为严厉，但仅靠债务无法取代股东支付作为应对经理人代理冲突的手段。
