## Abstract

This study investigates the trading behavior of institutional and individual investors around both finn-specific news releases in the Wall Street Journal and macro-economic announcements. For the firm-specific news releases we find that investors conduct a high degree of trading around news releases, especially earnings and dividend news. Institutions buy and sell on both good and bad news, while individual investors only trade on good news. The length of the news article (visibility) is also an important attribute to motivate individual investor trading. Lastly, both institutions and individuals buy large firms after good economic news and sell large firms after bad economic news. The trading of small firms does not appear to be motivated by macro-news. (C) 2001 Elsevier Science B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

本研究考察了机构投资者和个人投资者围绕《华尔街日报》中公司特定新闻发布以及宏观经济公告的交易行为。对于公司特定新闻发布，我们发现投资者围绕新闻发布进行大量交易，尤其是盈利和股利新闻。机构在好消息和坏消息上都进行买卖，而个人投资者只在好消息上交易。新闻文章的长度（能见度）也是激励个人投资者交易的一个重要属性。最后，在好的经济新闻之后，机构和个人都买入大型企业，在坏的经济新闻之后卖出大型企业。小型企业的交易似乎并非由宏观新闻驱动。
