## Abstract

This study examines the extent to which agency-based models and asymmetric information theories explain dividend smoothing around the world. Tests on a cross-section of more than two thousand firms from twenty-four countries show that managers of firms with low market-to-book ratios and less cash engage in greater dividend smoothing. Further, firms with highly-concentrated ownership structure and strong corporate governance smooth dividends less. In addition, managers of firms in industries facing high levels of competition smooth dividends more. We also determine that the extent of legal protections provided to shareholders and the culture of the country in which the firm is incorporated, as well as tax regime, have additional explanatory power for dividend smoothing. Our results are most consistent with the simultaneous presence of agency and information asymmetry effects in the decision to smooth dividends. (C) 2014 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

本研究考察基于代理的模型和信息不对称理论在多大程度上能够解释世界各地的股利平滑。对来自24个国家两千多家企业的横截面进行的检验表明，市值账面比低且现金较少的企业的经理人进行更多的股利平滑。此外，所有权结构高度集中、公司治理强的企业股利平滑较少。另外，处于竞争激烈行业的企业的经理人股利平滑更多。我们还确定，为股东提供的法律保护程度、企业注册所在国的文化以及税收制度对股利平滑具有额外的解释力。我们的结果与代理效应和信息不对称效应在股利平滑决策中同时存在最为一致。
