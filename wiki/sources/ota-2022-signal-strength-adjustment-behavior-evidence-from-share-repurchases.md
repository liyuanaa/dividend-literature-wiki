## Abstract

This paper extends the signaling hypothesis by investigating the signal strength adjustment behavior with respect to the announcement of an open market repurchase (OMR). Given that an OMR is a non-binding commitment for the repurchasing firm, the stock market would likely scrutinize the credibility of the undervaluation signal from the OMR announcement of the firm. This may compel the manager to engage in various mechanisms in order to strengthen the undervaluation signal of the OMR announcement. This paper investigates whether managers of repurchasing firms would modify the terms of the OMR program when the simultaneous announcements of bad news threaten the credibility of the signal from the OMR announcements. Consistent with our signal strength adjustment hypothesis, we find that managers of repurchasing firms increase (shorten) the repurchase plan size (period) with the magnitude of bad news in the simultaneous announcements. Our results also show that the stock market reacts positively to the signal strength adjustments, indicating that they are informative to the market. These results hold after using various techniques to control for sample selection bias.(c) 2022 The Author(s). Published by Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文）

本文通过考察围绕公开市场回购（OMR）公告的信号强度调整行为，扩展了信号假说。鉴于OMR对回购企业而言是一项非约束性承诺，股票市场可能会审视企业OMR公告所发出的低估信号的可信度。这可能迫使管理者采取各种机制以强化OMR公告的低估信号。本文考察，当同时发布的坏消息威胁到OMR公告信号的可信度时，回购企业的管理者是否会修改OMR计划的条款。与我们的信号强度调整假说一致，我们发现回购企业的管理者会随同时公告中坏消息的程度而增加（缩短）回购计划的规模（期限）。我们的结果还表明，股票市场对信号强度调整作出正面反应，表明这些调整对市场具有信息含量。在使用各种技术控制样本选择偏差之后，这些结果依然成立。
