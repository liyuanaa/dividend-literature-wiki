## Abstract

This paper provides evidence of the association between a firm's investment opportunity set (IOS), director ownership, and corporate policy choices. Using a sample of growth and non-growth firms in an emerging Asian market, we find that the IOS theory has significant explanatory power in the financing, dividend, executive compensation, and leasing aspects of corporate policies. Growth firms have lower debt-to-equity ratios and dividend yields, pay higher cash compensation and bonus amounts to their top executives, and finance a higher proportion of their asset acquisitions through operating leases. We also find that director ownership moderates and counteracts the association between IOS and corporate policies. Our results are consistent with contracting theory predictions that high director ownership mitigates the need for incentive or bonus compensation plans in growth firms. (C) 2002 Elsevier B.V. ## 摘要（中文）

本文提供了企业投资机会集（IOS）、董事持股与公司政策选择之间关联的证据。使用一个新兴亚洲市场中成长型和非成长型企业的样本，我们发现IOS理论在公司政策的融资、股利、高管薪酬和租赁等方面具有显著的解释力。成长型企业具有较低的债务-股权比率和股利收益率，向其高管支付更高的现金薪酬和奖金金额，并通过经营租赁为更高比例的资产收购提供融资。我们还发现，董事持股会调节并抵消IOS与公司政策之间的关联。我们的结果与契约理论的预测相一致，即高董事持股会缓解成长型企业对激励或奖金薪酬计划的需求。
