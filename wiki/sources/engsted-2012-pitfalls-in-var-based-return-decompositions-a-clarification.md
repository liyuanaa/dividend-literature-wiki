## Abstract

We analyze the pitfalls involved in VAR based return decompositions. First, we show that recent criticism of such decompositions is misplaced and builds on invalid VAR models and erroneous interpretations. Second, we derive the requirements needed for VAR decompositions to be valid. A crucial - but often neglected - requirement is that the asset price needs to be included as a state variable in the VAR. In equity return decompositions this requirement is equivalent to including the dividend-price ratio in the VAR. Finally, we clarify the intriguing issue of the role of the residual component in return decompositions. In a properly specified first-order VAR, it makes no difference whether cash flow news or discount rate news is backed out residually, and it makes no difference whether both news components are computed directly or one of them is backed out residually. ## 摘要（中文）

我们分析了基于VAR的收益分解所涉及的陷阱。首先，我们表明近期对此类分解的批评是错误的，并且建立在无效的VAR模型和错误的解释之上。其次，我们推导了VAR分解有效所需的条件。一个关键但常被忽视的要求是，资产价格需要作为一个状态变量被纳入VAR。在股权收益分解中，这一要求等价于将股利-价格比纳入VAR。最后，我们澄清了关于残差成分在收益分解中作用这一引人入胜的问题。在一个正确设定的一阶VAR中，无论是现金流消息还是贴现率消息被残差性地反推出来都没有区别，无论是两个消息成分都被直接计算还是其中一个被残差性地反推出来也都没有区别。
