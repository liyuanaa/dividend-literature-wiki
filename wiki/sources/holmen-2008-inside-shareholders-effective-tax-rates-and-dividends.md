## Abstract

Using information collected from the Swedish tax authorities, we calculate insiders' actual effective tax rates on dividends. With this unique dataset, we find a significant negative cross-sectional relationship between insiders' effective tax rates and dividend payout. This result is consistent with a tax-induced clientele effect for dividends. We also look at the impact of large block trades on dividends. We find that when insiders with zero effective taxes sell blocks. subsequent dividend payments are significantly more likely to decrease. This provides evidence that large shareholders are adjusting dividends for their individual tax Situations. (C) 2008 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们利用从瑞典税务机关收集的信息，计算了内部人在股利上的实际有效税率。借助这一独特的数据集，我们发现内部人有效税率与股利派现之间存在显著的负向横截面关系。这一结果与由税收诱发的股利投资者群体效应相一致。我们还考察了大宗股权交易对股利的影响。我们发现，当有效税率为零的内部人出售大宗股权时，随后的股利支付显著更有可能减少。这提供了证据表明，大股东会根据其个人的税收状况来调整股利。
