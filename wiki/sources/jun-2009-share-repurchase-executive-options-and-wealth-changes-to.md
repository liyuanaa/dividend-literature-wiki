## Abstract

We test the signaling and wealth transfer hypotheses around the announcement of share repurchases using a recent and larger sample of data than previously examined while employing a methodology designed to enhance the power of our tests. Disentangling the wealth transfer and signaling hypotheses is difficult; they are not mutually exclusive and can have opposite effects for bondholders. Wealth transfers decrease bondholder wealth while positive signals increase it; the combined result obscures tests of each hypothesis. By focusing on sub-samples where signaling is more and less likely to be present we increase our ability to isolate the separate effects. In addition to traditional tests of wealth effects, we feature information inherent in the correlation of wealth changes to equity and debt. Our results are generally consistent with the positive signaling effect of stock repurchases, but also provide some support for wealth transfer. Our work also emphasizes the importance of trying to disentangle the various hypotheses. In the subset of option funding repurchases. where signaling effects are less likely, the positive correlation of wealth changes between stockholders and bondholders is completely eliminated. Bond ratings are much more likely to be upgraded in samples without executive options which is precisely where the signaling effects are expected to be concentrated. Firms with weaker shareholder rights experience greater bondholder wealth losses at the announcement of stock repurchases. (C) 2008 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们围绕股份回购公告，使用比先前考察的更近期和更大规模的数据样本，并采用一种旨在增强检验效力的方法，检验了信号传递假说和财富转移假说。将财富转移假说和信号传递假说区分开来是困难的；它们并不相互排斥，且对债券持有人可能产生相反的影响。财富转移减少债券持有人财富，而正向信号增加债券持有人财富；两者的综合结果模糊了对每个假说的检验。通过聚焦于信号传递更可能和更不可能存在的子样本，我们提高了分离各自效应的能力。除了传统的财富效应检验外，我们还突出了股权与债务财富变化相关性中所蕴含的信息。我们的结果总体上与股票回购的正向信号传递效应相一致，但也为财富转移提供了一些支持。我们的工作还强调了试图区分各种假说的重要性。在期权融资回购这一子集中（那里信号传递效应更不可能存在），股东与债券持有人财富变化之间的正相关被完全消除。在没有高管期权的样本中，债券评级被上调的可能性大得多，而这恰恰是预期信号传递效应集中的地方。股东权利较弱的企业在股票回购公告时经历更大的债券持有人财富损失。
