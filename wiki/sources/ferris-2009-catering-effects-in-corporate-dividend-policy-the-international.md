## Abstract

This study tests for the international presence of dividend catering across a sample of twenty-three countries. We find evidence of catering among firms incorporated in common law countries but not for those in civil law nations. Catering persists even after controlling for the effect of the firm's lifecycle. We conclude that when the legal regime and its accompanying set of investor protections permit, investors force dividends from managers, but they also attempt to extract such payouts indirectly by placing a high value on dividend paying firms. The relative failure of civil law firms to cater might be explained by idiosyncratic behaviors in the consumption of the private benefits of control or a lack of interest in responding to temporary market misevaluations of their equity. ## 摘要（中文）

本研究在涵盖二十三个国家的样本中检验股利迎合在国际上的存在。我们发现，在普通法国家注册的企业中存在迎合的证据，但在成文法国家的企业中则没有。即使在控制了企业生命周期的影响之后，迎合仍然存在。我们得出结论，当法律制度及其相应的一套投资者保护允许时，投资者会迫使管理者派发股利，但他们也试图通过对派息企业给予高估值来间接获取此类派现。成文法国家企业相对未能迎合，或许可以用其在控制权私人收益消费方面的特异行为、或缺乏对其股权暂时性市场错误估值作出回应的兴趣来解释。
