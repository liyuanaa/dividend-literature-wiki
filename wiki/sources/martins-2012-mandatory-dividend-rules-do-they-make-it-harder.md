## Abstract

What are the costs and benefits of mandatory dividend rules? On the one hand, they make it harder for controlling shareholders to divert corporate assets. On the other hand, they reduce the internal funds available for firms to invest, possibly leading to the loss of valuable projects. To assess this trade-off, we look at investment and dividend decisions in a sample of public firms in Brazil. We show that a significant fraction of these firms use loopholes of Brazil's mandatory dividend rules to avoid paying dividends. And yet, the dividend rules are effective. They help explain why the average dividend yield in Brazil is higher than in the U.S., without making it harder for firms to invest. (C) 2012 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

强制股利规则的成本和收益是什么？一方面，它们使控股股东更难挪用公司资产。另一方面，它们减少了公司可用于投资的内部资金，可能导致有价值项目的损失。为评估这一权衡，我们考察巴西上市公司样本中的投资和股利决策。我们表明，其中相当一部分公司利用巴西强制股利规则的漏洞来规避支付股利。然而，股利规则是有效的。它们有助于解释为何巴西的平均股利收益率高于美国，同时又不会使公司更难进行投资。
