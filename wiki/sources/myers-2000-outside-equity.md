## Abstract

Equity financing is modeled when cash flows and asset values are not verifiable. Investors have enforceable property rights to the firm's assets, but cannot prevent insiders (managers or entrepreneurs) from capturing cash flow Insiders must co-invest and pay in each period a dividend sufficient to ensure outside investors' participation for at least one more period. Intervention by the investors must be limited by an agreement with insiders or by costs of collective action. Basic models are extended to show why firms go public and why agency costs necessarily arise when the act of investment is not immediately verifiable.

## 摘要（中文）

本文对现金流和资产价值不可验证时的股权融资进行了建模。投资者对企业资产拥有可执行的财产权，但无法阻止内部人（管理者或企业家）攫取现金流。内部人必须共同投资，并在每一期支付足以确保外部投资者至少再参与一期的股利。投资者的干预必须受到与内部人的协议或集体行动成本的限制。基本模型被加以扩展，以说明为什么企业会上市，以及为什么当投资行为无法立即被验证时，代理成本必然会产生。
