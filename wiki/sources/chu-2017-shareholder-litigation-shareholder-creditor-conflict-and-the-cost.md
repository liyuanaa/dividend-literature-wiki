## Abstract

I study how shareholder litigation affects the cost of bank loans via its impact on the distribution of bankruptcy estate and the conflict of interests between shareholders and creditors. Using a natural experiment based on a ruling by the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals, I find that increasing the difficulty of class action suits decreases loan spreads. The effect is stronger for firms with higher institutional ownership, which is consistent with the argument that class actions suits help shareholders extract wealth from creditors when the firm is in bankruptcy. Further analysis shows that the effect is weaker for firms with stronger creditor protection in bankruptcy. (C) 2017 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我研究了股东诉讼如何通过其对破产财产分配以及股东与债权人之间利益冲突的影响，来作用于银行贷款成本。基于第九巡回上诉法院一项裁决所构成的自然实验，我发现提高集体诉讼的难度会降低贷款利差。对于机构持股较高的企业，这一效应更强，这与如下论点相一致：当企业陷入破产时，集体诉讼有助于股东从债权人那里攫取财富。进一步分析表明，对于在破产中债权人保护更强的企业，这一效应较弱。
