## Abstract

We develop measures of the management quality of firms and make use of a unique sample of hand-collected data to examine the relationship between the reputation and quality of,I firm's management and its financial and investment policies, a relationship that has so far received little attention in the literature. We hypothesize that better and more reputable managers are able to convey the intrinsic value of their firm more credibly to outsiders, thus reducing the information asymmetry facing their firm in the equity market. Given this, firms with better and more reputable managers will have more access to the equity market, so that we expect lower leverage ratios for these firms. In addition, they will have less need to signal using dividends, so that they will have lower dividend payout ratios. Further, since better managers are likely to select better projects (having a larger net present value (NPV) for any given scale) and to implement them more ably, higher management quality will also be associated with higher levels of investment. We present evidence consistent with the above hypotheses. Our direct tests of the relationship between management quality and asymmetric information also indicate that higher management quality leads to a reduction in the extent of information asymmetry facing I firm in the equity market.

## 摘要（中文）

我们构建了企业管理质量的度量指标，并利用一个独特的手工收集数据样本来考察企业管理层的声誉和质量与其融资和投资政策之间的关系——这一关系迄今在文献中鲜有关注。我们假设，更优秀、声誉更好的经理人能够更可信地向外部人传达其企业的内在价值，从而降低其企业在股权市场面临的信息不对称。鉴于此，拥有更优秀、声誉更好的经理人的企业将更容易进入股权市场，因此我们预期这些企业的杠杆率更低。此外，它们用股利进行信号传递的需要更少，因此其股利支付率会更低。进一步地，由于更优秀的经理人更可能选择更好的项目（在任何给定规模下具有更大的净现值NPV）并更有能力地实施这些项目，更高的管理质量还将与更高的投资水平相关联。我们提供了与上述假设一致的证据。我们对管理质量与信息不对称之间关系的直接检验还表明，更高的管理质量导致企业在股权市场面临的信息不对称程度降低。
