## Abstract

We employ a difference-in-differences estimation approach to examine the impact of stakeholder orienta-tion on corporate payout policy. The empirical test exploits the enactment of US state-level constituency statutes, which allow directors to consider stakeholders and long-term interests in corporate decision making. We find that firms incorporated in states that have adopted constituency statutes significantly reduce share repurchases, whereas the effects of statute enactment on total payout and dividend payments are marginal and insignificant, respectively. We further show that the negative statute effect on share repurchases is more pronounced for firms that are in financial distress or are close to default, and firms in consumer-focused and high-polluting industries. Overall, our findings indicate that promoting stakeholder orientation can have a significant impact on corporate payout decisions. (c) 2020 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们采用双重差分估计方法来考察利益相关者导向对企业支付政策的影响。实证检验利用了美国州层面利益相关者法规的颁布，这些法规允许董事在企业决策中考虑利益相关者和长期利益。我们发现，在采纳了利益相关者法规的州注册的企业显著减少股份回购，而法规颁布对总支付和股利支付的影响分别是微弱的和不显著的。我们进一步表明，法规对股份回购的负向效应对陷入财务困境或接近违约的企业、以及消费者导向和高污染行业的企业更为显著。总体而言，我们的发现表明，促进利益相关者导向能够对企业支付决策产生显著影响。
