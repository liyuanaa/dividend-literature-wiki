## Abstract

We find that minority shareholders' voting opposition to dividend proposals is associated with signifi-cantly higher cash dividend payout in the following year for stocks listed in Shanghai Stock Exchange. When minority shareholders' voting opposition increases, the likelihood and frequency of regulatory penalties increase. The reverse happens after firms increase dividend payout. Minority shareholders' vot-ing opposition has a stronger positive effect on cash dividend payout when they post more messages in stock forums and when independent directors express dissenting opinions. The effect is weaker if the board chair or CEO has political connections. Minority shareholders' voting opposition does not have sig-nificant effects on the growth rate of earnings and the level of earnings management. But we do find evidence that minority shareholder voting opposition reduces expropriation by major shareholders. The evidence supports a hypothesis that regulators use the minority shareholder voting results to screen out possible cases of shareholder oppression.(c) 2022 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们发现，对于在上海证券交易所上市的股票，中小股东在股利提案上的投票反对与次年显著更高的现金股利支付相关。当中小股东的投票反对增加时，监管处罚的可能性和频率上升。企业在增加股利支付后则出现相反情况。当中小股东在股票论坛上发布更多信息、以及当独立董事表达异议时，其投票反对对现金股利支付的正向影响更强。若董事长或首席执行官具有政治关联，则该影响较弱。中小股东的投票反对对盈利增长率和盈余管理水平没有显著影响。但我们确实发现证据表明，中小股东投票反对减少了大股东的侵占。这一证据支持了这样一个假说：监管者利用中小股东的投票结果来筛查可能存在的股东压迫案例。

## Key Findings

- Minority shareholders' voting opposition to dividend proposals is associated with significantly higher cash dividend payout in the following year; for each one standard deviation increase in Dissent, Dividend2A_After increases by 7.4% relative to the mean.
- Higher minority voting opposition increases the likelihood and frequency of regulatory penalties by 33% per one SD increase in Dissent; conversely, if firms subsequently increase dividend payout, regulatory penalties decrease by 43.3%.
- The effect of voting opposition on dividends is stronger when minority shareholders post more messages in stock forums (SMCover) and when independent directors express dissenting opinions (IndDissent), and is weaker when the board chair or CEO has political connections.
- The primary mechanism through which dividend increases are generated is a reduction in expropriation by major shareholders (intercorporate tunneling via other receivables), not changes in earnings management or perk consumption.
- The evidence supports the vote disclosure screening hypothesis: regulators use minority shareholder voting results to screen for firms with potential governance problems, inducing preemptive dividend increases to avoid scrutiny.
