## Abstract

This paper investigates whether female independent directors are more likely to impose high dividend payouts. We find evidence that firms with a larger fraction of female directors on their board have greater dividend payouts. This finding is robust to alternative econometric specifications, and alternative measures of dividend payouts and female board representation. The positive effect of board gender composition on dividends remains when we employ propensity score matching, the instrumental variable approach, and difference-in-differences approach to address potential endogeneity concerns. Furthermore, we find that board gender composition significantly increases the dividend payout only for firms with weak governance, suggesting that female directors use dividend payouts as a governance device. 本文考察女性独立董事是否更可能推动高股利支付。我们发现证据表明，董事会中女性董事比例较大的企业股利支付更高。这一发现对替代性计量经济学设定、以及股利支付和女性董事会代表性的替代性度量都是稳健的。在我们采用倾向得分匹配、工具变量方法和双重差分方法来处理潜在内生性顾虑时，董事会性别构成对股利的正向影响依然存在。此外，我们发现，董事会性别构成只对治理薄弱的企业显著增加股利支付，这表明女性董事将股利支付用作一种治理工具。
