## Abstract

Debt-financed share buybacks generate positive short-term and long-run abnormal stock returns. Leveraged buyback firms have more debt capacity, higher marginal tax rate, lower excess cash and lower growth prospects ex ante, increase leverage and reduce investments more sharply ex post than cash-financed buyback firms. Firms that are over-levered ex-ante are associated with lower returns and real investments following leveraged buybacks. The lower announcement returns of over-levered firms are concentrated on firms with weaker corporate governance. The evidence is consistent with leveraged buybacks enabling firms to optimize their leverage, on average benefiting shareholders. The benefits decrease with a firm's leverage ex ante. ## 摘要（中文）

债务融资的股票回购能产生正的短期和长期异常股票收益。相比现金融资的回购企业，杠杆回购企业事前拥有更大的债务容量、更高的边际税率、更低的过剩现金和更低的成长前景，事后则更大幅度地增加杠杆并削减投资。事前杠杆过高的企业在杠杆回购后与较低的收益和实际投资相关。杠杆过高企业较低的公告收益集中于公司治理较弱的企业。这些证据与如下观点相一致：杠杆回购使企业能够优化其杠杆，平均而言使股东受益。但随着企业事前杠杆的升高，这些收益递减。
