## Abstract

This paper examines changes in corporate dividend policy around the introduction of a dividend imputation tax system. This represented a significant change to the Australian tax framework and allows us to test the effect of differential taxation on corporate dividend policy. Consistent with the tax preference for the distribution of dividends, we find dividend initiations, all dividend payout measures and dividend reinvestment plans increased with the introduction of dividend imputation. Similarly we find that gross dividend payouts are more volatile under dividend imputation. Finally, we find that the increase in dividend payout and initiations differs across firms. In particular, we find that the higher the level of available franking tax credits the higher the firm's gross dividend payout and the more likely the firm is to initiate a dividend. ## 摘要（中文）

本文考察在引入股利抵免税制前后企业股利政策的变化。这代表了澳大利亚税收框架的一次重大变化，使我们能够检验差异化征税对企业股利政策的影响。与股利分配的税收偏好相一致，我们发现，随着股利抵免的引入，股利启动、所有股利支付度量以及股利再投资计划都有所增加。同样，我们发现，在股利抵免制下，总股利支付更加波动。最后，我们发现，股利支付和启动的增加在不同企业间存在差异。特别是，我们发现，可用的税收抵免额水平越高，企业的总股利支付就越高，企业启动股利的可能性也越大。
