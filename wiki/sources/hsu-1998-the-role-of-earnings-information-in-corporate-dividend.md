## Abstract

This paper examines the role of earnings information in the determination of dividend policy. I We decompose accounting earnings into permanent and transitory components and postulate that dividend policy is driven by sustainable permanent earnings. Two measures of permanent earnings are proposed. The first is a permanent earnings variable extracted from accounting earnings by a random-level shift ARMA model. The second measure is stock price times cost of capital. These two permanent earnings measures are employed in the Marsh-Merton (1987) model to explain corporate dividend behavior and their performance is compared. A generalized friction method is adopted for empirical estimation to account for stepwise dividend movements over time. Results show that the permanent earnings measure extracted from accounting earnings data explains dividend dynamic behavior better than stock price.

## 摘要（中文）

本文考察了盈余信息在股利政策决定中的作用。我们将会计盈余分解为持久性成分和暂时性成分，并假设股利政策由可持续的持久性盈余所驱动。本文提出了两种持久性盈余的度量。第一种是通过一个随机水平移动ARMA模型从会计盈余中提取的持久性盈余变量。第二种度量是股价乘以资本成本。这两种持久性盈余度量被用于Marsh-Merton（1987）模型来解释公司股利行为，并比较其表现。实证估计采用广义摩擦法以考虑股利随时间的逐步变动。结果表明，从会计盈余数据中提取的持久性盈余度量比股价更好地解释了股利的动态行为。
