## Abstract

Share repurchases help alleviate agency costs of surplus cash by restricting management's scope to waste corporate resources. But why do self-interested managers agree to disgorge surplus cash in the first place? This study examines the intervening effect of managerial monitoring and incentive alignment mechanisms on the decision to distribute excess cash through a share repurchase. Findings indicate that repurchases substitute for cash retention decisions that would otherwise prove costly for shareholders, and that better managerial incentive alignment and closer monitoring by external shareholders are important factors stimulating such payouts. ## 摘要（中文）

股份回购有助于通过限制管理层浪费公司资源的空间来缓解盈余现金的代理成本。但为什么自利的经理人一开始会同意吐出盈余现金呢？本研究考察了经理人监督和激励协同机制对通过股份回购分配多余现金这一决策的中介影响。研究结果表明，回购替代了那些原本会对股东造成代价的现金留存决策，并且更好的经理人激励协同以及外部股东更密切的监督是刺激此类支付的重要因素。
