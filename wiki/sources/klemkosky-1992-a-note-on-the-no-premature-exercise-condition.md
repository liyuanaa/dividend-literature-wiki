## Abstract

It is shown that Merton's sufficient condition for no rational premature exercise of a dividend payout unprotected American call option is frequently misunderstood and used incorrectly in the finance literature when applied to the multiple dividend case. This is especially true in papers we receive to review for journals. The purpose of this note is to clarify understanding of the no premature exercise condition.
