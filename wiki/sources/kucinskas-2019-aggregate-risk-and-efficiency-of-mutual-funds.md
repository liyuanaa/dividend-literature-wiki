## Abstract

This paper studies the welfare properties of mutual funds in a Diamond-Dybvig economy with two sources of aggregate risk: (i) shocks to long-term investment returns; and (ii) shocks to aggregate liquidity demand. Mutual funds are inefficient when the long-term investment is risky. Mutual funds improve upon autarky by introducing a wedge between the market and technological rates of return. However, the wedge distorts the decisions of consumers not facing liquidity shocks, and the resulting equilibrium is inefficient. If only aggregate liquidity demand is subject to shocks, mutual funds can implement the social optimum (even when liquidity demand is not directly observable) by means of a price-contingent dividend policy. ## 摘要（中文）

本文研究在具有两种总量风险来源的Diamond-Dybvig经济中共同基金的福利性质：(i) 对长期投资回报的冲击；以及 (ii) 对总量流动性需求的冲击。当长期投资有风险时，共同基金是无效率的。共同基金通过在市场回报率与技术回报率之间引入一个楔子而改进了自给自足（autarky）状态。然而，该楔子扭曲了不面临流动性冲击的消费者的决策，由此产生的均衡是无效率的。如果只有总量流动性需求受到冲击，那么共同基金可以通过一种依赖于价格的股利政策来实现社会最优（即使流动性需求不可直接观察）。
