## Abstract

We examine a distribution that is taxed as a capital gain rather than as a dividend. Since the distribution induces a realized capital gain while the price change is an unrealized gain, ex-day return behavior provides evidence of the value of tax-timing capital gains. We show that investors are compensated 7 cent in unrealized gains for each dollar of realized capital gains, that is, $1 of realized capital gains is equivalent to 93 cent of unrealized gains. An investor with a tax rate on realized gains of 15% has an effective tax rate on unrealized capital gains of 8.6%.

## 摘要（中文）

我们考察一种被作为资本利得而非股利征税的分配。由于该分配诱发了已实现资本利得，而价格变化是未实现利得，除权日的收益行为为资本利得税收择时（tax-timing）的价值提供了证据。我们表明，投资者每获得一美元已实现资本利得会在未实现利得上得到7美分的补偿，也就是说，1美元的已实现资本利得等价于93美分的未实现利得。一个对已实现利得税率为15%的投资者，其未实现资本利得的有效税率为8.6%。
