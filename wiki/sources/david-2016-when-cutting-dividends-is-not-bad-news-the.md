## Abstract

We provide evidence on optional stock dividends, a mechanism that allows shareholders to choose between cash dividends and the equivalent number of new shares in lieu of dash. We find that, in contrast to dividend cuts, shareholders do not view this option as bad news. When firms offer optional stock dividend in lieu of cash dividends, the market does not react negatively. Facing the choice between cash and stock dividend, shareholders choose 55% of the total dividend in the form of stock dividend. Our findings suggest that firms that are more committed to paying dividends are more likely to offer optional stock dividends to their shareholders. (C) 2016 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们就可选股票股利（optional stock dividends）提供证据，这是一种允许股东在现金股利与等额新股（以代替现金）之间进行选择的机制。我们发现，与股利削减相反，股东并不将这一选择权视为坏消息。当公司提供可选股票股利以代替现金股利时，市场不会做出负面反应。在面对现金与股票股利的选择时，股东选择将股利总额的55%以股票股利的形式领取。我们的发现表明，更致力于支付股利的公司更可能向其股东提供可选股票股利。
