## Abstract

We study how stock option grants are funded through share repurchases under conditions of option exercisability and moneyness. Using daily repurchase disclosures by U.K. firms, we corroborate our hypothesis that driven by flexibility, firms repurchase early in an option schedule while options are out-of-money and before becoming exercisable. Our findings show that when daily stock prices are below weighted average option exercise price and when options are not immediately exercisable, firms (a) increase daily repurchase volume (value), (b) increase repurchase frequency, and (c) have lower relative repurchase prices. We further evidence this by examining the change in treasury regulation that enabled firms to hold on to repurchased shares rather than cancelling them. Our findings show a strong support for option funding motives in the post-treasury regulation period when repurchase flexibility is greater. (C) 2020 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们研究在期权可行权性和价内价外状态的条件下，如何通过股份回购来为股票期权授予提供资金。使用英国企业的每日回购披露数据，我们印证了如下假设，即出于灵活性的驱动，企业在期权计划早期、当期权处于价外且尚未变得可行权之前进行回购。我们的发现表明，当每日股价低于加权平均期权行权价格且期权尚不可立即行权时，企业 (a) 增加每日回购量（价值），(b) 提高回购频率，以及 (c) 拥有更低的相对回购价格。我们通过考察一项使企业能够持有回购股份而非将其注销的库存股监管变化，进一步证明了这一点。我们的发现为库存股监管后回购灵活性更大的时期内的期权融资动机提供了有力支持。
