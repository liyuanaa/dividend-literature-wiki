## Abstract

Recent evidence demonstrates that corporate payout policy has shifted from the nearly exclusive use of dividend payout to the inclusion of stock repurchase, primarily through open markets. This trend has been attributed to the tax advantages associated with repurchase relative to dividends. In this paper, we introduce personal taxation and stock repurchase to reexamine the relation between returns and the bid-ask spread. Our model provides insight into the nature of this relation. Tests performed using NYSE, AMEX, and NASDAQ data provide empirical support of our theoretical conclusions. We conclude that the firm's choice of payout policy influences the relation between returns and spreads. (c) 2005 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

近期证据表明，企业支付政策已经从几乎完全使用股利支付转变为纳入股票回购，主要通过公开市场进行。这一趋势被归因于回购相对于股利的税收优势。本文引入个人税收和股票回购，重新考察收益与买卖价差之间的关系。我们的模型为这一关系的本质提供了洞见。使用纽交所、美交所和纳斯达克数据进行的检验为我们的理论结论提供了实证支持。我们的结论是，企业对支付政策的选择影响收益与价差之间的关系。
