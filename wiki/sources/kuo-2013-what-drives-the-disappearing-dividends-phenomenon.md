## Abstract

We study the determinants of dividend payout policy and examine the role of liquidity, risk and catering in explaining the changes in propensity to pay. Our results indicate that risk plays a major role in firms' dividend policy. The evidence substantiates from a large sample of firms representing 18 countries over the sample period from 1989 to 2011. For firms in the US, France, UK and Other European markets, liquidity is additionally an important determinant of dividend policy. We find that, although catering incentives persist only among firms in common law countries and not in civil law countries, after adjusting for risk there is little support for catering theory even among firms incorporated in common law countries. Our results indicate that catering incentives reflect the risk-reward relationship in the changing propensity to pay dividends. (C) 2013 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

我们研究股利支付政策的决定因素，并考察流动性、风险和迎合在解释派息倾向变化中的作用。我们的结果表明，风险在企业股利政策中发挥了主要作用。这一证据得到了代表18个国家、样本期为1989年至2011年的大样本企业的证实。对于美国、法国、英国和其他欧洲市场的企业，流动性还是股利政策的一个重要决定因素。我们发现，尽管迎合动机仅在普通法国家的企业中持续存在而在成文法国家中不存在，但在对风险进行调整后，即便在普通法国家注册的企业中，迎合理论也几乎得不到支持。我们的结果表明，迎合动机反映了派息倾向变化中的风险—回报关系。
