## Abstract

Agency theory suggests that outside shareholders prefer higher dividend payouts in order to reduce the free cash flows of firms that are under the insiders' control. Our study investigates the effects of mutual funds, typically the most important and influential type of outside shareholder, on firms' dividend payouts in China during the period from 2003 to 2011. We find that mutual funds influence firms to pay higher cash dividends. The results are consistent with the predictions from exit theory. The effects are more pronounced in firms controlled by state and regional governments and in firms with relatively higher free cash flows. We also find evidence that the mutual funds' effects are stronger when their investment horizon is longer and the ownership interest is larger. Other institutional investors, such as banks, insurance companies, and securities companies have a lower exit threat and do not have an influence on firms' cash dividend payments or financial performances. ## 摘要（中文）

代理理论认为，外部股东偏好更高的股利支付，以减少受内部人控制的企业的自由现金流。我们的研究考察了共同基金（通常是最重要、最有影响力的外部股东类型）在2003年至2011年期间对中国企业股利支付的影响。我们发现，共同基金促使企业支付更高的现金股利。这些结果与退出理论的预测相一致。该效应在由国家和地方政府控制的企业以及自由现金流相对较高的企业中更为显著。我们还发现证据表明，当共同基金的投资期限较长、持股比例较大时，其影响更强。其他机构投资者，如银行、保险公司和证券公司，退出威胁较低，对企业的现金股利支付或财务业绩没有影响。
