## Abstract

This paper proposes and tests a new method to extract the value of corporate voting rights from market prices of American-style single-stock options. The method models voting-right values as non-cash dividends and backs them out via numerical optimization from prices of equity options. Simulation experiments show that the method is accurate and outperforms existing option-based approaches by reducing their measurement error from 17.2% to 1.57% in terms of root mean squared errors and almost eliminates their bias. The paper also contributes an empirical analysis of corporate voting-right values in European companies in the time period between 2003 and 2010. Voting rights have an annualized average value of 0.37% of the share price and are significantly worth more in months in which either ordinary or extraordinary general meetings take place but no single shareholder holds a majority stake in the company. Finally, voting values are higher in companies incorporated in French-civil-law countries (France and the Netherlands) than in German-civil-law countries (Germany and Switzerland). ## 摘要（中文）

本文提出并检验了一种从美式单一股票期权的市场价格中提取公司投票权价值的新方法。该方法将投票权价值建模为非现金股利，并通过数值优化从股票期权价格中反推出来。模拟实验表明，该方法准确，并优于现有基于期权的方法：以均方根误差衡量，它将现有方法的测量误差从17.2%降至1.57%，并几乎消除了其偏误。本文还对2003年至2010年间欧洲公司的投票权价值进行了实证分析。投票权的年化平均价值为股价的0.37%，并且在召开普通或特别股东大会但公司无单一股东持有多数股权的月份中显著更高。最后，在法国民法法系国家（法国和荷兰）注册的公司中，投票权价值高于德国民法法系国家（德国和瑞士）的公司。
