## Abstract

We first extend Baker and Wurgler's (2004a) catering theory of dividends to share repurchases. Consistent with the notion that firms cater to investor demand for share repurchases, we report evidence that the market's time-varying repurchase premium positively affects firms' choice to repurchase shares. Next, we use the catering behavior as a novel framework for testing the dividend substitution hypothesis. Consistent with the notion that managers consider dividends and share repurchases to be substitute payout mechanisms, we find that the dividend premium negatively affects the repurchase choice, whereas the repurchase premium negatively affects the choice to pay dividends. ## 摘要（中文）

我们首先将贝克和沃格勒（Baker and Wurgler，2004a）的股利迎合理论扩展到股份回购。与企业迎合投资者对股份回购的需求这一观念相一致，我们报告的证据表明，市场随时间变化的回购溢价正向影响企业回购股份的选择。接着，我们将迎合行为用作检验股利替代假说的新框架。与管理者将股利和股份回购视为替代性支付机制这一观念相一致，我们发现股利溢价负向影响回购选择，而回购溢价负向影响支付股利的选择。
