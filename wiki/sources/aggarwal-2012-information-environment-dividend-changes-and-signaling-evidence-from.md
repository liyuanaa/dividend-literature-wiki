## Abstract

While theory suggests that dividends can be an important credible signal for firm performance, prior studies have been unable to provide strong evidence of dividend signaling among publicly listed U. S. companies. One potential explanation for this inconsistency between theory and empirical evidence is that the cross-sectional variation in information asymmetry across U. S. firms is insufficient to provide adequate test power. In this study, we revisit the link between dividend signaling and firms' information environment by examining the dividend behavior of foreign firms that cross-list on the U. S. stock market in the form of American Depository Receipts (ADRs). Our evidence suggests that ADR firms with poorer information environments have stronger incentives to adopt dividend increases as a credible signaling device. We also find that such firms experience an increase in one-year-ahead earnings and a decline in systematic risk following a dividend increase. Additional analysis shows that ADR firms have fewer other information channels compared to similar U. S. firms. Overall, we provide evidence consistent with the importance of dividend signaling, especially for firms with poorer information environments.

## 摘要（中文）

尽管理论认为股利可以成为公司业绩的一个重要且可信的信号，但先前的研究一直无法在美国公开上市公司中提供股利信号传递的有力证据。理论与实证证据之间这种不一致的一个潜在解释是，美国公司之间信息不对称的横截面差异不足以提供充分的检验效力。在本研究中，我们通过考察以美国存托凭证（ADR）形式在美国股票市场交叉上市的外国公司的股利行为，重新审视股利信号传递与公司信息环境之间的联系。我们的证据表明，信息环境较差的ADR公司有更强的动机采用增加股利作为一种可信的信号传递手段。我们还发现，此类公司在增加股利后，其未来一年的盈余上升，系统性风险下降。进一步分析表明，与相似的美国公司相比，ADR公司拥有的其他信息渠道更少。总体而言，我们提供的证据与股利信号传递的重要性相一致，尤其对于信息环境较差的公司而言。
