## Abstract

A number of empirical studies have reached the conclusion that stock: price volatility cannot be fully explained within the standard dividend discount model This article proposes a resolution based upon a model that contains both a random supply of risky assets and finitely lived agents who trade in a multiple security environment As the analysis shows there exist 2(K) equilibria when K securities trade. The low volatility equilibria have properties analogous to those found in the infinitely lived agent models of Campbell and Kyle (1991) and Wang (1993, 1994). In contrast; the high-volatility equilibria have very different characteristics. Within the high-volatility equilibria very large price variances can be generated with very small supply shocks Adding securities to the economy further reduces the required supply shocks. Using previously established empirical results the model can reconcile tbe data with supply shocks that are less than 10% as large as observed return shocks. These results are shown to hold even when the dividend process is mean reverting.

## 摘要（中文）

许多实证研究得出结论，股价波动无法在标准股利贴现模型内得到完全解释。本文提出一种基于如下模型的解决方案：该模型既包含风险资产的随机供给，又包含在多证券环境中交易的有限存活期主体。分析表明，当K种证券交易时，存在2的K次方个均衡。低波动率均衡具有与Campbell和Kyle（1991）以及Wang（1993、1994）无限存活期主体模型中所发现的类似性质。相比之下，高波动率均衡具有极为不同的特征。在高波动率均衡内，极小的供给冲击就能产生极大的价格方差。向经济中增加证券会进一步减小所需的供给冲击。利用先前确立的实证结果，该模型能够用不到观测收益冲击10%大小的供给冲击来调和数据。这些结果被证明即使在股利过程均值回复时也成立。
