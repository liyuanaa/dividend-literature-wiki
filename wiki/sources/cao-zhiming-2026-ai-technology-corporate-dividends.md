# 人工智能技术应用与企业股利分配：影响与机制 (2026)

> **Paper claim** — AI technology adoption by Chinese listed firms significantly reduces both the probability and intensity of cash dividend distributions, operating through three channels: increased R&D investment, elevated risk-taking, and enhanced ESG performance.

## Abstract

在数智时代，人工智能（AI）已成为企业转型升级、实现可持续发展的重要战略工具。基于2013—2023年A股上市公司的数据，实证检验了人工智能赋能应用对企业股利分配政策的影响及其机制。研究发现，人工智能赋能应用对企业股利发放的可能性和强度均有显著负向影响。机制分析表明，人工智能赋能应用通过增强研发投入、提高承担风险水平以及提升ESG绩效等三个方面抑制企业股利的分配。

*English summary:* Using data from A-share listed companies from 2013 to 2023, this study empirically tests the impact and mechanism of AI technology application on corporate dividend distribution policies. The research finds that the application of AI technology inhibits the probability and intensity of corporate dividend distribution. Mechanism analysis shows that AI technology inhibits dividend distribution through: strengthening R&D investment, improving risk-taking levels, and enhancing ESG performance. The inhibitory effect is more significant in small-scale enterprises, non-capital-intensive enterprises, and enterprises with low industry competition.

## Key Findings

- AI technology adoption (measured by ln(keyword frequency + 1) from annual reports) significantly reduces both dividend payout probability (Div1) and intensity (Div2) at 1% significance; the negative association holds after IV estimation (lagged AI disclosure), Heckman selection correction, and PSM.
- Three mechanism channels confirmed via mediation analysis (Bootstrap, 1000 replications, 95% CI excludes zero): AI → increased R&D intensity (RD) → lower dividends (H1a); AI → higher risk-taking (Risk) → lower dividends (H1b); AI → improved ESG performance → lower dividends (H1c).
- Heterogeneity by firm size: the inhibitory effect is significant only for small-scale enterprises; large firms show no significant effect, consistent with large firms having more stable cash flows and better access to external financing.
- Heterogeneity by capital intensity: the effect is concentrated in non-capital-intensive enterprises; capital-intensive firms' AI adoption may complement rather than crowd out dividends due to scale economies and stable asset bases.
- Heterogeneity by factor market development: AI inhibits dividend intensity (Div2) at both high and low levels of factor market development, but inhibits dividend probability (Div1) only when factor market development is high.
- Alternative AI proxies (AI_Invest: AI fixed asset investment intensity; AI_Adopt: fixed asset value / employee count above sector median) yield consistent results.
