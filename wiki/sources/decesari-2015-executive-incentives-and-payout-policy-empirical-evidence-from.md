## Abstract

We investigate how corporate payout policy is influenced by executive incentives, i.e. stock and option holdings, stock option deltas and stock-based pay-performance sensitivity for 1,650 publicly listed firms from the UK, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands and Spain, over the period from 2002 to 2009. Our results show that executive stock option holdings and stock option deltas are associated with lower dividend payments in our sample of European countries, where we do not observe any presence of dividend protection for executive stock options. We find that this relationship is mainly driven by exercisable stock options and by options that are in the money. Additionally, we observe that executive stock option holdings and stock option deltas have a negative impact on total payout, suggesting that executives do not substitute share repurchases for dividends. Furthermore, the fraction of share repurchases in total payout increases as executive stock option holdings and stock option deltas increase. Finally, our results show that executive share ownership and stock-based pay-performance sensitivity may mitigate agency conflicts by significantly increasing the level of total payout. ## 摘要（中文）

我们考察企业支付政策如何受到高管激励（即股票和期权持有、股票期权德尔塔以及基于股票的薪酬-业绩敏感性）的影响，样本为2002年至2009年期间来自英国、德国、法国、意大利、荷兰和西班牙的1,650家公开上市企业。我们的结果表明，在我们的欧洲国家样本中，高管股票期权持有和股票期权德尔塔与更低的股利支付相关，而我们在这些样本中未观察到任何对高管股票期权的股利保护。我们发现，这一关系主要由可行权期权和实值期权驱动。此外，我们观察到高管股票期权持有和股票期权德尔塔对总支付具有负面影响，这表明高管不会用股份回购替代股利。而且，随着高管股票期权持有和股票期权德尔塔的增加，回购在总支付中所占的比例上升。最后，我们的结果表明，高管持股和基于股票的薪酬-业绩敏感性可能通过显著提高总支付水平来缓解代理冲突。
