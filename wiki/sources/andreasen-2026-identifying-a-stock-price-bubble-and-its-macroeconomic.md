## Abstract

We construct a present-value model with a rational bubble identified using stock market data and survey expectations. The model captures the 1970s "nifty-fifty" bubble, the dot-com bubble, the bubble prior to the 2007-2009 recession, a COVID-19 bubble, and a recent bubble possibly linked to artificial intelligence. When bubble-adjusting the price-dividend ratio, we no longer find explosive dynamics and excess volatility in stock prices. To examine the macroeconomic implications of a bubble, we use the bubble innovation as an instrument in a structural vector autoregression. The results show that a positive bubble shock is expansionary and leads to a higher monetary policy rate.

## 摘要（中文）

我们构建了一个现值模型，其中的理性泡沫是使用股票市场数据和调查预期来识别的。该模型捕捉了20世纪70年代的“漂亮五十”（nifty-fifty）泡沫、互联网泡沫、2007-2009年衰退之前的泡沫、COVID-19泡沫，以及一个近期可能与人工智能相关的泡沫。当对价格—股利比进行泡沫调整后，我们不再发现股价中的爆炸性动态和过度波动。为考察泡沫的宏观经济含义，我们在结构性向量自回归中将泡沫创新用作工具变量。结果表明，一个正向泡沫冲击是扩张性的，并导致更高的货币政策利率。
