## Abstract

This paper examines the impact of risk-sensitive Basel regulations on debt financing of firms around the world. It investigates how firms cope with the impact through adjustments to their financing sources and capital investments. We find that the implementation of Basel II regulations is associated with reduced credit availability for lower-rated firms. Such firms mitigate the shortage in bank credit through increased reliance on accounts payable, lower payouts to shareholders, and reduced capital investments. The impact of the capital regulation is lower in countries that rely on the internal ratings-based approach. The key results are robust to controls for banking crises, bank-specific controls, and the inclusion of loan-level information. The findings of this paper substantially contribute to the understanding of the real effects of risk-sensitive bank capital regulations. (c) 2021 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

本文考察了风险敏感型巴塞尔监管规则对全球企业债务融资的影响。文章探究了企业如何通过调整其融资来源和资本投资来应对这一影响。我们发现，巴塞尔II监管规则的实施与评级较低企业的信贷可得性下降相关。这类企业通过增加对应付账款的依赖、减少对股东的派现以及削减资本投资来缓解银行信贷的短缺。在采用内部评级法的国家，资本监管的影响较小。这些主要结果在控制了银行业危机、银行特定控制变量以及纳入贷款层面信息后依然稳健。本文的发现对理解风险敏感型银行资本监管的实际效应做出了重要贡献。
