## Abstract

Bank capital ratios have increased steadily since the financial crisis. For a sample of 101 large banks from advanced and emerging economies, retained earnings account for the bulk of their higher risk-weighted capital ratios, with reductions in risk weights playing a lesser role. On average, banks continued to expand their lending in real terms, though lending contracted among European banks. Lower dividend payouts and (for advanced economy banks) wider lending spreads have contributed to banks' ability to use retained earnings to build capital. Banks that came out of the crisis with higher capital ratios and stronger profitability were able to expand lending more. (C) 2016 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.

## 摘要（中文）

自金融危机以来，银行资本比率稳步上升。对于来自发达和新兴经济体的101家大型银行样本，留存收益构成了其较高风险加权资本比率的主要来源，而风险权重的下降所起的作用较小。平均而言，银行继续按实际值扩大放贷，尽管欧洲银行的放贷有所收缩。较低的股利派现以及（对发达经济体银行而言）更宽的放贷利差，促成了银行能够利用留存收益来积累资本。在危机后拥有较高资本比率和较强盈利能力的银行，能够更多地扩大放贷。
