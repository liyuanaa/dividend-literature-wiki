## Abstract

If you're in the business of service delivery, investment in the training and development of your staff is one of the keys to your company's success. But what's the best way to design and implement your investment? In 1994, Fidelity Institutional Retirement Services Company (FIRSCo) needed to ensure that its rapidly expanding staff maintained the company's high levels of customer satisfaction. The solution, according to Ellyn McColgan, formerly an executive vice president of FIRSCo and now the president of Fidelity Investments Tax-Exempt Services Company, was to reach out to its service associates with a powerful new model for training and development called Service Delivery University. SDU is a virtual university with a content-based core curriculum and five colleges that focus on business concepts and skills. It is driven by three principles. First, all training must be directly aligned with the company's strategic and financial objectives and focused on customer needs. Second, service delivery is a profession and should be taught as such. And finally, professional development should be the primacy responsibility of line managers rather than the human resources department. McColgan explains how FIRSCo overcame resistance to this sweeping change in employee education. (Time was one obstacle: each associate receives 80 hours of training per year.) In addition, the author discusses the fine art of measuring the success of a program like SDU. She finds that the company's investment has paid dividends to the staff, to the organization as a whole, and to FIRSCo's customers.

## 摘要（中文）

如果你从事服务交付行业，对员工培训与发展的投资是公司成功的关键之一。但设计和实施这种投资的最佳方式是什么呢？1994年，富达机构退休服务公司（FIRSCo）需要确保其迅速扩张的员工队伍保持公司高水平的客户满意度。据Ellyn McColgan（曾任FIRSCo执行副总裁，现任富达投资免税服务公司总裁）所言，解决方案是通过一种名为"服务交付大学"的强大新型培训与发展模式来触及其服务人员。SDU是一所虚拟大学，拥有以内容为基础的核心课程和五个专注于商业概念与技能的学院。它由三条原则驱动。第一，所有培训必须与公司的战略和财务目标直接对齐，并聚焦于客户需求。第二，服务交付是一门专业，应作为专业来教授。最后，专业发展应主要是一线经理而非人力资源部门的责任。McColgan解释了FIRSCo如何克服对这一员工教育重大变革的抵触。（时间是障碍之一：每位员工每年接受80小时培训。）此外，作者讨论了衡量像SDU这样的项目成败的精妙艺术。她发现，公司的这项投资已经向员工、向整个组织以及向FIRSCo的客户带来了回报。
