## Abstract

This paper investigates the relation between bank dividends and bank risk over the period 1984-2011, and assesses the existence of risk-taking and risk-shifting in the US commercial banking sector subject to regulatory regime changes. The introduction of PCA in 1992 and TARP in 2008 constitute significant regulatory regime changes, and provide the necessary framework to explore whether regime-dependent risk-shifting or risk-taking is present. We find strong evidence of risk-shifting and risk-taking over the post-PCA regime spanning the period 1992-2008. We interpret this evidence as indication of ineffectiveness of PCA in controlling risk-taking and risk-shifting. The finding of risk-taking just prior to the recent financial crisis suggests that risk-taking may be a factor contributing to this crisis. As risk-taking and risk-shifting are important aspects of bank behavior (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, 2009), these results are of interest to bank regulators and important to Basel III. ## 摘要（中文）

本文考察1984—2011年期间银行股利与银行风险之间的关系，并评估在受监管制度变化影响下的美国商业银行业中是否存在风险承担和风险转移。1992年引入的及时纠正措施（PCA）以及2008年引入的问题资产救助计划（TARP）构成了重大的监管制度变化，并提供了探究是否存在依赖于制度的风险转移或风险承担的必要框架。我们在涵盖1992—2008年期间的PCA后制度中发现了风险转移和风险承担的有力证据。我们将这一证据解读为PCA在控制风险承担和风险转移方面无效的迹象。在近期金融危机之前恰好发现风险承担，这表明风险承担可能是导致这场危机的一个因素。由于风险承担和风险转移是银行行为的重要方面（巴塞尔银行监管委员会，2009），这些结果对银行监管者具有意义，并对巴塞尔协议III具有重要性。
